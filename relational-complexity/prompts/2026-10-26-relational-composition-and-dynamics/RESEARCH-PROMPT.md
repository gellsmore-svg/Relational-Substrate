## 2. Scientific distinctions to preserve

Before selecting a new investigation, explicitly distinguish:

1. **Constituent state**: the specified relationships that exist or are active.
2. **Derived observation**: a label, count, graph, cover or further relation calculated from constituent state, adding no independent choice.
3. **Additional independent state**: at least two full states share the same constituent projection and differ in a separate coordinate or configuration.
4. **Compositional operation**: a typed rule mapping systems plus an interface or relation into another well-defined system. A name or grouping operation alone does not imply dynamical composition.
5. **Event grammar**: allowable elementary updates, including single changes, joint changes, interface updates, membership changes and no-ops, each with explicit clock.
6. **Dynamical law**: support, raw event weights, event identities, normalised transition probabilities and halt semantics.
7. **Effective description**: a projection with exact Markov closure, one that requires hidden state/history, or one that is undefined under the chosen quotient criteria.
8. **Equivalence scope**: original-coordinate support, a coordinate permutation, arbitrary invertible recoding, injective embedding into a larger state space, kernel conjugacy, exact quotient, labelled-event equivalence or equivalence under a changed observation clock.

Do not claim a novel independent entity from an extra bit, or a new physical mechanism from a joint event. Equally, do not identify all relational structure with raw binary coordinates and thereby exclude alternatives by definition.

In every mathematical claim, identify *which* state mapping, clock, observed distinctions and event semantics are preserved. Mathematical equivalence of aggregate transition kernels need not preserve labelled event identities or original carrier interpretations.

## 3. Candidate directions — invitations, not requirements

### A. Evolving relationships among relational structures

Start with clearly specified relational subsystems, interfaces and composition operators. Examples worth comparing include:

- An identified shared interface versus duplicated copies that interact, agree, disagree or synchronise.
- A changing family of relational subsets or block memberships; distinguish *actual family change* from activation of an otherwise fixed cover.
- Two composites participating in another relationship, with state or update rules defined for that further participation.
- Relational constraints on the **interface between systems** as distinct from constraints on constituent edges.
- Operations that admit another iteration of composition without simply being a repeated relabelling or a trivial product.

A meaningful construction must state its input/output types, composition rule, valid states, update events, clock, constraints, observations and what a subsequent composition can actually act upon.

Compare plausible alternatives that **might reduce** to old grammars and ones that **might not**, without engineering a preferred outcome. If all tried alternatives reduce, say precisely under what encoding and which information survives. If no well-defined operator has yet emerged, a rigorous typed counterexample or comparison can itself be the completed research increment.

Do not automatically give a new mathematical structure the name "level", "higher-order entity", "agency" or "emergence". Define any such claim before using it.

### B. Recoding boundaries and what they do *not* explain

The accepted XOR proof states that a collection of *distinct global XOR update directions* can all become separate coordinate flips under **one invertible binary-linear map** exactly when the directions are independent. This concerns support/coordinate recoding.

Independently useful extensions might explore:

- State-dependent availability of update directions versus the global direction set.
- Different weight functions on the same support and consequent changes in exact projections.
- State recodings not restricted to binary-linear maps, with proven necessary or sufficient conditions on bounded graph families.
- Hypercube-embedding obstructions other than the known triangle and excess-common-neighbour examples, without simply searching for a larger number.
- Graph isomorphism or an injective embedding that preserves one-step support but **not** carrier, event or weight semantics.
- Models with genuine state-dependent update *masks*, contrasted with state-dependent *rates* on a fixed mask set.
- Comparison of labelled-event equivalence and ordinary Markov-kernel equivalence on mathematically meaningful families.

Be careful: dependence of directions is only a failure of the **specific linear coordinate-axis recoding**. It is not a universal failure of all nonlinear embeddings. An invertible recoding need not make coordinate processes independent. Do not rerun the two old circuit examples as discoveries.

This portfolio is optional; choose it only if it advances understanding rather than padding the existing XOR study.

### C. When an observation retains enough dynamics

For composed or coupled systems, distinguish two separate problems:

**(i) Representational simplification.** Is there an equivalent description under a declared transformation?

**(ii) Predictive closure.** Does an observation retain the exact transition law for every full state represented by an observed state?

Use exact strong-lumpability checks and correctly typed quotient maps. If a quotient fails, show the block-row witness; do not fill nonexistent transition rows or call an approximate, initial-distribution-specific result a strong quotient.

Possible mathematically informative comparisons:

- Two systems with the same static relational structure but different hidden-state-dependent transition laws.
- Fixed versus evolving omitted coordinates.
- Equal one-step aggregate kernels with different event-level histories.
- Exact two-stage reductions versus a direct coarse reduction whose intermediate observation is non-Markov.
- Whether an interface identification is invariant under one coupling and leaks under another.
- Finite memory requirements for a projected process, if the model and probability assumptions make that question precise.

The last completed analytic study proved a positive-ray criterion for a **particular independent-direction recoding** with inactive coordinates; do not re-prove it as a new result. A broader or genuinely different class must have a distinct mathematical question and a scoped answer.

### D. Dynamical influence, interventions and alternative event clocks

If the selected mathematics makes an influence question meaningful, formulate the contrast **before computing**:

- Initial-state perturbation with matched full-state controls.
- Matched alteration of one event rule or admissible transition.
- The effects of coupling two previously independent relational systems.
- A typed intervention operator with explicit assumptions, if introducing one is warranted.
- An observation of multiple declared microsteps, explicitly distinguished from a single joint event.

Report exact finite-horizon distributions where tractable. Keep observational dependence, initial-state sensitivity, interventional influence, one-step reachability and longer-horizon effects separate. A path in a dependency diagram is not a dynamic cascade.

No predetermined timescale, cascade depth, propagation direction or growing-complexity metric.

## 4. Choose with Open-Thetical judgement

Do not interpret the preceding directions as a to-do list. During orientation, consider several genuinely different candidate questions and record a concise, qualitative decision using the five established vectors:

- **Consequence** — Which distinct explanations or mathematical constructions can the outcomes separate?
- **Readiness** — Are types, semantics, controls and verification possible without hidden assumptions?
- **Leverage** — How broadly could this inform the larger relational-composition programme?
- **Cognitive/computation cost** — Is it a suitable increment for current compute, model credits and state size?
- **Personal research significance** — Does the inquiry address relationships participating in other relationships, rather than convenient numerical curiosities?

These vectors are **not a numeric scoring formula** and need not produce a ranking. Choose the highest-information next question you can justify. An analytic proof, a counterexample, a precise reduction, a coherent type analysis plus independently verified consequence, or a frozen and verified experimental round can each constitute a completed increment **when it answers a real question**.

Negative outcomes, reducibility and a reasoned stop are admissible. Do not stop immediately after mere code preparation or a frozen plan while claiming completed research.
