## Reassessment decision procedure

Before future execution, inspect the live Git history, `PROGRESS.md`, coverage registers, the plans and any already-committed results. Assess each of the three plans independently by:

1. **Consequence:** What alternative mathematical possibilities would its possible outcomes separate?
2. **Readiness:** Is the tested mathematical operation well-defined, independently checkable and bounded?
3. **Leverage:** Does the result connect meaningfully with other open investigations?
4. **Cognitive / computation cost:** Does the expected information repay the research effort?
5. **Personal significance / research relevance:** Does it serve the larger RRC question of compositional relational dynamics without preordaining an outcome?

These are **qualitative assessment vectors**, not a weighted score or a mandatory form.

If a plan still promises meaningful information, execute **its original frozen specification unchanged** and complete the exact verification, provenance and interpretation cycle. If it has been superseded or its value is low, document why in a **new dated decision record**, identify it as `retired-unexecuted` or `superseded-unexecuted`, and leave its original file and freeze intact. If its intended experiment needs changing, create a **different round identifier with a new freeze**, not a patched version of the old plan.

If a new model simply follows a different, more informative inquiry, this record's **deferred** status is sufficient: no artificial cancellation or proof of redundancy is required.
