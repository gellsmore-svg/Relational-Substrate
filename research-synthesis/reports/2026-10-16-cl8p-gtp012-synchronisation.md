Let A be GT's continuous-time generator, pi its stationary law, and C_K its edge-count projection. With L_pi(x|k)=pi(x)/p_k, the coarse generator is B=L_pi A C_K. Its birth rate is J[k,k+1]/p_k. NK realizes this same birth-death generator with each absent-slot rate B[k,k+1]/(M-k), and analogous present-slot rates. Those rates are unique **within that uniform per-slot class**.

This does not identify NK with repeated conditional reset of the material graph. In GT, pi(x|k) need not be uniform. Even when it is uniform, NK preserves the incidence of the current graph and changes one edge; an independent uniform target-layer reset has other destinations.

A parameter-free three-slot witness: from {0}, an upward single toggle reaches {0,1} or {0,2}, never {1,2}. An independent uniform size-two target-layer readout gives {1,2} positive mass. Thus fine-state support distinguishes the constructions without a model sweep.
