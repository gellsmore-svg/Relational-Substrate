# Cross-programme connections

Only records in `ledger/connections.jsonl`. Every one carries an explicit review status. Human acceptance is implicit in the round trip (`docs/cadence.md`): it accepts a reported recommendation at its stated decision category and never upgrades it. Evidential types compare formal objects on eight dimensions (see each review record). Independence is stated because GT and CL share 8G lineage.

**Decision category** is the category the agent recommended (rubric 03); human acceptance keeps it. **Review status** says who has reviewed it. A connection resting on a closed but **unreviewed** round is marked from the manifest's per-source review metadata and stays `qualified` (or `candidate`) even when human-accepted.

## Rationale

**X::0001** (tested_structural_analogy; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). Both programmes exhibit, exactly and on their own side, a passage-type statistic that differs between two processes whose matched stationary quantities are equal: CL ordered-sibling MFPTs differ (446 slower, 274 faster) at identical source/target occupancy, clock and Kac recurrence; GT flexible-motif episode lifetimes differ by up to x25 between PCOMP and a comparator with the same stationary law, density, mean rate and edge lifetime. Map: matched occupancy <-> matched stationary law/density; Kac recurrence control <-> CT edge-lifetime calibration; MFPT <-> entrance-episode lifetime.

**X::0002** (epistemic_compatibility; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). Both programmes conclude, from their own exact results, that one-time stationary occupancy is not a sufficient test: CL requires multi-time statistics, conditional process tests and Markov-order checks with explicit clock and projection; GT requires a comparator that declares where activity sits. The recommendations are compatible and mutually reinforcing as method, not as evidence about any mechanism.

**X::0003** (explicit_contrast; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). Both programmes find that conclusions depend on the clock, but the operations are not the same. CL's pair-event epoch counts only pair events and hides triad events, so a first-order full process looks memoryful on the epoch (projection plus time change). GT's continuous time and jump clock share one event sequence and differ only in holding-time weighting, so they change orderings (relaxation, sorting) but cannot create apparent memory. Treating them as one phenomenon would be an error; the shared theme is that the clock belongs to the estimand.

**X::0004** (explicit_contrast; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). An apparent contradiction ('reweighting can break reversibility' versus 'every single-witness composition is reversible') dissolves on the formal objects. CL Generation 1 rules are labelled: each acts on one named edge and reads present/absent literals on other named edges (constraint-lab/docs/constraint-dsl.md). GT-F12 covers only label-covariant templates whose weight depends on witness counts. Label covariance plus single-witness structure gives a Gibbs potential; labelled multi-literal rules need not. GT-F13 shows label-covariant two-witness templates also break reversibility. The boundary is label covariance and witness arity.

**X::0005** (shared_implementation_assumption; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). GT's relational potential (pi proportional to m^S) is the Gibbs law of a multiplicative-weight template. That multiplicative composition is CL-T01, inherited with the 8G DSL and kernel builder on which the GT branch was created. The agreement is shared implementation, not independent evidence.

**X::0006** (qualifies; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). CL-T01 places the zero (prohibit) weight outside the logarithmic algebra. GT-F54 shows that, for the measured stationary observables (partner index, flux), the hard ban sits at the continuous end of the soft ladder 1/2, 1/4, 1/9, 1/16, 0. 'Outside the algebra' therefore does not imply 'discontinuous in every observable'. The support still changes (one-way transitions, GT-F51), so the qualification is limited to those observables.

**X::0007** (epistemic_compatibility; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). CL separates exact dependence on current higher-order structure from memory of the initial triad, which the visible pair process forgets. GT finds that a selected hub's advantage (a memory of an initial selection) decays toward zero in every law while current-state rules still shape the decay rate. The two are compatible instances of retention being distinct from current dependence; no shared formal object is asserted.

**X::0008** (qualifies; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). CL's H axis concerns rules that read history at identical complete current state. GT-Q22 asks only for an observable computed on trajectories; no rule reads it. CL's distinction therefore qualifies GT-Q22: answering it does not require or test a history axis, and the 8G compilation gate is not engaged.

**X::0009** (tested_structural_analogy; HUMAN-ACCEPTED (at the stated decision category)). Both are 'matching X fixes Y' identities that turn an apparent discriminator into a control. CL-T88: starting from the stationary conditional law on D, E[tau_D^+] = 1/pi(D), so equal occupancy forces equal Kac recurrence. GT-F79: E_pi[q] = 2M J_edge by exchangeability, so matching density and mean rate forces equal CT edge present-lifetime. Map: pi(D) <-> edge occupancy; Kac recurrence <-> edge present-lifetime; both follow from stationary flux balance across a set boundary.

**X::0010** (explicit_contrast; AGENT-REVIEWED (awaiting human acceptance)). GT PCOMP's large ND lifetime excess disappears against layer-traffic-matched NK, whereas CL sibling MFPT differences survive full adjacent-(P,h1)-matched Kpi. This qualifies a mechanism reading of X::0001, not its occupancy/process analogy. PCOMP within-class durability can still differ; pooled lifetime is not durability.

**X::0011** (epistemic_compatibility; AGENT-REVIEWED (awaiting human acceptance)). CL intentionally uses stationary class-conditioned sources. GT post-renewal survival uses a first-hit law. Both are legitimate for their distinct tasks: GT's stationary-restriction warning does not invalidate CL's original source convention. Future CL post-hit continuation would need an explicit first-hit law.

**X::0012** (requires_scope_alignment; AGENT-REVIEWED (awaiting human acceptance)). Both comparators aggregate stationary directed flow under a declared projection. GT NL is a pool-exchangeable single-toggle realization preserving directed circulation, not CL's repeated conditional reset of h2. Restricted uniqueness and the different clocks, supports and sources prevent a common fine operator claim. PATHF's residual collapses while PATHH's survives, so finer matching does not have one universal effect.
