## Claim

Both programmes find that conclusions depend on the clock, but the operations are not the same. CL's pair-event epoch counts only pair events and hides triad events, so a first-order full process looks memoryful on the epoch (projection plus time change). GT's continuous time and jump clock share one event sequence and differ only in holding-time weighting, so they change orderings (relaxation, sorting) but cannot create apparent memory. Treating them as one phenomenon would be an error; the shared theme is that the clock belongs to the estimand.
