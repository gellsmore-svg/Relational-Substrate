# Round trips and recommendations

Human acceptance of **synthesis interpretations** is implicit in the round trip: the author's next instruction ratifies the items listed for the latest closed round, unless the author objects. Since AD-0004, only items displayed here at the round's closing commit can be ratified. Ratification never upgrades a decision category.

The round trip **never** gives scientific review of a CL/GT generation, and **never** gives consent to publish private historical material (that needs an explicit `publication_consent` decision).

**Policy amendment AD-0004** (2026-10-15): round-close records each recommendation with its scope, qualification and whether it rests on an unreviewed round; generated/round-trips.md displays every open recommendation; ratification refuses any item that the round trip's closing commit did not display; three acceptances stay separate: synthesis interpretation (round trip); scientific review of a CL/GT generation (never through the round trip); consent to publish private historical material (an explicit publication_consent decision only). Grandfathered: RT-0001, RT-0002.

Audit note: RT-0001 was opened retrospectively at the moment the implicit-acceptance rule was introduced and ratified in the same session (AD-0002). Its 15 items had been shown to the author only in prose (reports/2026-10-15-initial-federation.md, the PR #8 description and the generated links and metaphor views), not as a structured list before the instruction that ratified them. AD-0002 stands and is not rewritten; it accepted synthesis interpretations at their reported categories only. It is not scientific review of CL 008N or GT-P009 and not publication consent. RT-0002 listed no items.
