## 0. What kind of thing we are modelling (read first)

This is an **experimental ontological model based on coherence**, drawing on
others' topology/soliton research for *language*, not for its ontology. The
substrate is **not material**: it does not fit the human *send → transmit →
receive* perception model, so it cannot be directly experienced — only its
downstream effects can. **Coherence is therefore the only admissible mode of
description.** We never describe the substrate as a substance or a mechanism; we
describe it as *coherence and its topology*.

Two consequences fix the whole method:

- **Progress is measured as containment, not fit.** More information that drives
  coherence ⇒ greater containment of the admissible pattern-space ⇒ higher
  likelihood of success. Success in this phase is *how tightly the space of
  admissible patterns has been contained by robust, assumption-stripped
  constraints* — not a quantitative match (that comes far later, and only
  parameter-free).
- **The default topological state of the substrate is darkness — a rest
  configuration, not an absence.** The ground state is not empty space, nor vague
  potential: it is the substrate *at rest* — a definite, fully-present,
  unperturbed configuration (bounded metaphor: a taut, unperturbed trampoline
  skin — a real tensioned medium, simply not yet displaced). It is a **thing**,
  and it is precisely what *enables* light — and potentially E&M — to form
  topologically upon it. It is *unrevealed* only in that it carries no closed
  structure yet, so no identity invariant is instantiated — **not** in the sense
  of being empty. **The rest state carries a rest orientation** — a uniform
  **nematic axis** (an *unoriented* director, target RP², in a 3D bulk) at every
  locale, all aligned but with *no preferred head* (bounded image: a trampoline
  skin with a brushed grain whose fibres have no arrow). Darkness is therefore
  *perfect uniform alignment*: maximal
  orientational order, zero topological charge; undifferentiated only in that it
  holds no texture or knot. This rest orientation is the substrate's **magnetic
  constraint geometry in its ground state** (the *M* of the old AMS name; Axiom
  M1). Structure is *revealing*: coherence rising, first as displacement and then
  as closure, against the rest state. (The CBO ground-state framing sits behind
  this; kept bounded here.)

## 1. Seven working principles (the lens at every stage)

1. **Coherence-only description.** No substance, no mechanism, no contact-force
   language. Only coherence and its topology.
2. **Darkness is the default — a substrate at rest, not an absence.** The ground
   state is a *thing*: the substrate in its definite, unperturbed rest
   configuration (bounded metaphor: a taut, unperturbed trampoline skin). It is
   *unrevealed* only in that it holds no closed structure yet, so no identity
   invariant is instantiated — not in the sense of being empty or vague. It
   **carries a rest orientation** (a uniform **nematic axis** — unoriented
   director, target RP², 3D bulk — the substrate's ground magnetic geometry);
   revealing is any texture that *departs from uniform alignment*. The rest state is what *enables* revealing: light forms as a
   perturbation of it, and (open) E&M may form topologically upon it too. Every
   construction starts here and asks what minimal information *reveals* the next
   structure.
3. **Information → coherence → containment → success.** Each step must either
   *contain* (rule a pattern out) or *correlate* (link two patterns). Nothing is
   added that does neither.
4. **Strip buried assumptions.** Every borrowed model is mined for its
   background-free, coherence-native pattern; its material / metric / mechanical
   scaffolding is discarded before use.
5. **Correlate patterns with patterns.** Confidence comes from the *same* robust
   pattern surviving independent strippings across unrelated models (convergence),
   not from any single model's authority.
6. **Background-independence guardrail.** No pre-given space. Relations first; any
   "space," "distance," or "field-at-a-point" must be *emergent*, never assumed.
7. **Ontology before model; bounded metaphor.** A metaphor (knot, ribbon, twist)
   is scaffolding used *after* the coherence claim, removable once the structure
   stands.

## 4. Guidelines for constructing thought experiments

1. **Start from darkness.** Every experiment opens at zero revealed coherence and
   asks what minimal information *reveals* the next structure.
2. **Only background-free quantities.** Linking, twist, writhe, helicity,
   invariance-under-transformation. No distances, no field-at-a-point, no forces.
3. **Each step earns its place** by *containing* (ruling a pattern out) or
   *correlating* (linking two patterns) — and says which.
4. **Invariants over magnitudes** in this phase. We ask *what is discrete/
   conserved/handed*, not *how big*.
5. **No fitted parameters, ever.** A step that needs a tuned coefficient is a
   calibration and is rejected (this is the exact prior-cycle failure).
6. **State the falsifier in advance.** Each experiment names what result would
   break the pattern.
7. **Keep a containment ledger** (§6): after each experiment, record what was
   contained and what correlated. That ledger *is* the phase's deliverable.

## 5. The series of topological thought experiments (T0–T7)

A graded, conceptual ladder — each adds containment, none is numeric yet.

  substrate's definite unperturbed rest configuration — a *thing*, fully present

  structure so no identity invariant is instantiated. It is what *enables* the
  later revealings. *Contains:* the ground state (a real rest medium; identity
  register empty; not empty space). *Falsifier:* if a stable identity invariant
  could exist without any departure from rest, the rest-default is wrong.
- **T1 — First revealing (light / propagation, Op A).** The minimal coherent
  *displacement* of the rest state — a perturbation that propagates, route-bearing
  but *unclosed* (no closure, so no identity invariant). Establishes that
  *revealing precedes identity*, and that the rest skin's perturbability is what
  carries light — its oscillation is where E&M first appear, in *transient* form.
  *Correlates:* darkness→light with Operation A; rest-state perturbability with the
  transient (open) category.
- **T2 — First closure (the vorton, Op D).** A route that returns and **self-links**:
  the minimal nonzero linking integer. Identity is born. *Falsifier / test:* does
  discreteness (integer linking) fall out **without a tuned threshold**? *(This is
  the charge-quantisation test of the proposal, met first as a thought experiment.)*
- **T3 — Twist vs. writhe (Op C vs. Op B).** Hold linking fixed; trade twist for
  writhe. *Predict:* the held-twist component behaves as **magnetism** (static,
  dissipationless, handed); the writhe/route component behaves as **electrical
  reconfiguration**. *Correlates:* Lk = Tw + Wr with the operation split.
- **T4 — Handedness = polarity.** Reverse the twist handedness. *Predict:*
  polarity sign flips and **nothing else** changes ("north/south = opposite
  chirality of the same held configuration"). *Falsifier:* if reversal needs extra
  structure beyond a sign flip.
- **T5 — Two coupled circulations = E&M complementarity.** Toroidal ↔ poloidal.
  *Predict:* dissipation-free exchange (resonance) between the complementary
  modes; neither is primary.
- **T6 — Coupling two vortons (secondary topology / bond).** Minimal coherent
  coupling of two linked structures. *Ask:* what invariant governs binding? First
  reach toward molecules — the exact place the prior cycle only ever *calibrated*.
- **T7 — Light meets vorton (admissibility).** Re-cast the existing admissibility
  walkthroughs: an open (unlinked) transient meeting a closed (linked) structure,
  resolved by **coherence-compatibility** (admitted / returned / stored), not by
  energy. Salvages `research/ams-light-vorton-interaction-*`.
