## 0. What kind of thing we are modelling (read first)

This is an **experimental ontological model based on coherence**, drawing on
others' topology/soliton research for *language*, not for its ontology. The
substrate is **not material**: it does not fit the human *send → transmit →
receive* perception model, so it cannot be directly experienced — only its
downstream effects can. **Coherence is therefore the only admissible mode of
description.** We never describe the substrate as a substance or a mechanism; we
describe it as *coherence and its topology*.

Two consequences fix the whole method:

- **Progress is measured as containment, not fit.** More information that drives
  coherence ⇒ greater containment of the admissible pattern-space ⇒ higher
  likelihood of success. Success in this phase is *how tightly the space of
  admissible patterns has been contained by robust, assumption-stripped
  constraints* — not a quantitative match (that comes far later, and only
  parameter-free).
- **The default topological state of the substrate is darkness — a rest
  configuration, not an absence.** The ground state is not empty space, nor vague
  potential: it is the substrate *at rest* — a definite, fully-present,
  unperturbed configuration (bounded metaphor: a taut, unperturbed trampoline
  skin — a real tensioned medium, simply not yet displaced). It is a **thing**,
  and it is precisely what *enables* light — and potentially E&M — to form
  topologically upon it. It is *unrevealed* only in that it carries no closed
  structure yet, so no identity invariant is instantiated — **not** in the sense
  of being empty. Structure is *revealing*: coherence rising, first as
  displacement and then as closure, against the rest state. (The CBO ground-state
  framing sits behind this; kept bounded here.)

## 1. Seven working principles (the lens at every stage)

1. **Coherence-only description.** No substance, no mechanism, no contact-force
   language. Only coherence and its topology.
2. **Darkness is the default — a substrate at rest, not an absence.** The ground
   state is a *thing*: the substrate in its definite, unperturbed rest
   configuration (bounded metaphor: a taut, unperturbed trampoline skin). It is
   *unrevealed* only in that it holds no closed structure yet, so no identity
   invariant is instantiated — not in the sense of being empty or vague. The rest
   state is what *enables* revealing: light forms as a perturbation of it, and
   (open) E&M may form topologically upon it too. Every construction starts here
   and asks what minimal information *reveals* the next structure.
3. **Information → coherence → containment → success.** Each step must either
   *contain* (rule a pattern out) or *correlate* (link two patterns). Nothing is
   added that does neither.
4. **Strip buried assumptions.** Every borrowed model is mined for its
   background-free, coherence-native pattern; its material / metric / mechanical
   scaffolding is discarded before use.
5. **Correlate patterns with patterns.** Confidence comes from the *same* robust
   pattern surviving independent strippings across unrelated models (convergence),
   not from any single model's authority.
6. **Background-independence guardrail.** No pre-given space. Relations first; any
   "space," "distance," or "field-at-a-point" must be *emergent*, never assumed.
7. **Ontology before model; bounded metaphor.** A metaphor (knot, ribbon, twist)
   is scaffolding used *after* the coherence claim, removable once the structure
   stands.

Each candidate is judged not by "is it right?" but by **what robust pattern

| **Field helicity** (∫ self-linkage; conserved in dissipationless flow) | **Self-linkage is conserved and dissipationless** — a field's "coherence with itself" | fluid/plasma medium; a carrier substance | Why held structure persists **without energy** (magnetism) |

| **Skyrme model** (integer winding = baryon number) | **Winding number → real conserved quantum number**; stability from a **balance of containment vs. dispersal** | chiral/pion material interpretation; background space; Derrick-stabilising term by hand | **Precedent only** (topological charge yields real physics) |

**Verdict on the anchor.** Anchor the *structural* language on **knot/ribbon
topology + helicity** — it is the most assumption-light and already coherence-
native (a linking number is nothing but "how coherently a structure is bound to
itself"). Use **Hopfion** only as the concrete field instantiation, **stripped of
its background-R³ assumption** (treat its field as a relational coherence
amplitude, not a value on pre-given space). Keep **Skyrme** as precedent, **GA**
for downstream expression, **relational/ZX** as guardrail + admissibility grammar.

## 3. The correlated core (what survives across *all* of them)

Strip every candidate and the *same* small pattern keeps reappearing — this
convergence is the phase's first real containment:

- **Identity = conserved self-linkage.** Discrete identity (charge, particle
  number, vorton kind) is an *integer linking/winding invariant*, not a substance
  and not a tuned threshold. Darkness = zero linking; a vorton = the first nonzero
  linking revealed against it.
- **Lk = Tw + Wr — the master decomposition.** One conserved linking integer
  splits, background-free, into:
  - **Twist** (held, static rotation) → **magnetism** (Operation C): a *held
    twist-geometry*, dissipationless because it is geometry not activity;
    **polarity = the handedness (sign) of the twist**.
  - **Writhe / route** (global coiling of the path) → **electricity /
    reconfiguration** (Operation B): the dynamic, re-routable component; **charge =
    the derivative asymmetry of this coiling**, its magnitude set by the integer.
- **Two coupled circulations → E & M complementary.** Toroidal ↔ poloidal =
  capacitive ↔ inductive = the book's complementary "forms of ordered
  constraint"; resonance is dissipationless exchange between them.
- **Stability = information-driven containment.** A structure persists where the
  tendency to *contain* (coherence, return) balances the tendency to *disperse* —
  the coherence reading of soliton stabilisation. More coherent information ⇒
  tighter containment ⇒ more stable identity. This *is* principle 3 made physical.
- **Light = revealing without linking** (Operation A): coherence that propagates,
  route-bearing but *unclosed* (zero linking) — the first revealing against
  darkness, prior to identity.

This core is background-free, coherence-native, and faithful to both books. It is
the scaffold everything below hangs on.

## 4. Guidelines for constructing thought experiments

1. **Start from darkness.** Every experiment opens at zero revealed coherence and
   asks what minimal information *reveals* the next structure.
2. **Only background-free quantities.** Linking, twist, writhe, helicity,
   invariance-under-transformation. No distances, no field-at-a-point, no forces.
3. **Each step earns its place** by *containing* (ruling a pattern out) or
   *correlating* (linking two patterns) — and says which.
4. **Invariants over magnitudes** in this phase. We ask *what is discrete/
   conserved/handed*, not *how big*.
5. **No fitted parameters, ever.** A step that needs a tuned coefficient is a
   calibration and is rejected (this is the exact prior-cycle failure).
6. **State the falsifier in advance.** Each experiment names what result would
   break the pattern.
7. **Keep a containment ledger** (§6): after each experiment, record what was
   contained and what correlated. That ledger *is* the phase's deliverable.

## 5. The series of topological thought experiments (T0–T7)

A graded, conceptual ladder — each adds containment, none is numeric yet.

- **T0 — Darkness (the substrate at rest).** Fix the ground state as the
  substrate's definite unperturbed rest configuration — a *thing*, fully present
  (bounded metaphor: a taut, unperturbed trampoline skin), carrying no closed
  structure so no identity invariant is instantiated. It is what *enables* the
  later revealings. *Contains:* the ground state (a real rest medium; identity
  register empty; not empty space). *Falsifier:* if a stable identity invariant
  could exist without any departure from rest, the rest-default is wrong.
- **T1 — First revealing (light / propagation, Op A).** The minimal coherent
  *displacement* of the rest state — a perturbation that propagates, route-bearing
  but *unclosed* (no closure, so no identity invariant). Establishes that
  *revealing precedes identity*, and that the rest skin's perturbability is what
  carries light — its oscillation is where E&M first appear, in *transient* form.
  *Correlates:* darkness→light with Operation A; rest-state perturbability with the
  transient (open) category.
- **T2 — First closure (the vorton, Op D).** A route that returns and **self-links**:
  the minimal nonzero linking integer. Identity is born. *Falsifier / test:* does
  discreteness (integer linking) fall out **without a tuned threshold**? *(This is
  the charge-quantisation test of the proposal, met first as a thought experiment.)*
- **T3 — Twist vs. writhe (Op C vs. Op B).** Hold linking fixed; trade twist for
  writhe. *Predict:* the held-twist component behaves as **magnetism** (static,
  dissipationless, handed); the writhe/route component behaves as **electrical
  reconfiguration**. *Correlates:* Lk = Tw + Wr with the operation split.
- **T4 — Handedness = polarity.** Reverse the twist handedness. *Predict:*
  polarity sign flips and **nothing else** changes ("north/south = opposite
  chirality of the same held configuration"). *Falsifier:* if reversal needs extra
  structure beyond a sign flip.
- **T5 — Two coupled circulations = E&M complementarity.** Toroidal ↔ poloidal.
  *Predict:* dissipation-free exchange (resonance) between the complementary
  modes; neither is primary.
- **T6 — Coupling two vortons (secondary topology / bond).** Minimal coherent
  coupling of two linked structures. *Ask:* what invariant governs binding? First
  reach toward molecules — the exact place the prior cycle only ever *calibrated*.
- **T7 — Light meets vorton (admissibility).** Re-cast the existing admissibility
  walkthroughs: an open (unlinked) transient meeting a closed (linked) structure,
  resolved by **coherence-compatibility** (admitted / returned / stored), not by
  energy. Salvages `research/ams-light-vorton-interaction-*`.

## 6. Containment ledger (living)

Filled as each T-experiment is worked. **Contained** = patterns ruled out / fixed;
**Correlated** = patterns linked. The tightness of this ledger — not any number —
is how this phase reports progress.

| T | Contained | Correlated |
|---|---|---|
| **T0** | ground state = the substrate *at rest* (a real tensioned medium), identity register empty, ≠ empty space | rest-state ↔ the ontology's continuous substrate; the enabling role ↔ why Ops A/D can occur at all |
| T1 | *(bridge)* first displacement = light; carries E&M in transient form; no closure | darkness→light ↔ Op A; perturbability ↔ transient/open category |
| **T2** | identity appears **only** with closure; identity = an **integer** topological invariant; discreteness forced by topology, not a tuned threshold | closure ↔ birth of identity ↔ first nonzero invariant; vorton ↔ "primary topology" (Ch. 17) |

### Worked entry — T0 (the substrate at rest)

- **Contained.** The ground state is the substrate *at rest*: a real, tensioned,
  fully-present configuration (trampoline-skin-at-rest), **not** empty space and
  **not** vague potential. The **identity register is empty** — no closed
  structure has formed, so no winding/linking invariant is instantiated. This
  fixes the precise meaning of "unrevealed" = *at rest / no closure*, retiring the
  misleading "zero linking." The rest state is **causally prior and enabling**: it
  is the condition of possibility for both propagation (light) and closure
  (vorton).
- **Correlated.** Rest-state ↔ the RS "continuous substrate" beneath primary
  topology; the *enabling* role ↔ why Operations A (propagation) and D (closure)
  are possible at all; "darkness" ↔ the CBO ground-state (bounded).
- **Open (flagged, deferred).** What are the rest state's *intrinsic* properties —
  a tension/stiffness analog? Is it uniform (maximally symmetric) or already
  textured — does it carry a rest orientation/director field? This is not idle:
  **the rest structure decides which invariant becomes available at T2** (a 2D
  winding vs a 3D Hopf/linking), and therefore whether the Lk = Tw + Wr split is
  usable at T3. *Bounded-metaphor note:* the trampoline image is good for
  *tension, rest, and perturbability*; it must be stripped of its embedding in a
  pre-given 3D room (principle 6, background-independence).
- **Falsifier.** A stable identity invariant existing with no departure from rest.

### Worked entry — T2 (first closure = the vorton)

- **Setup.** A displacement of the rest state that **returns and closes on
  itself**, and holds — the minimal vorton (Operation D). Enabled by T0's rest
  medium; revealed only after T1's capacity for displacement exists.
- **Contained.** Identity appears **only** with closure — never in the rest state,
  never in open propagation. The identity is a **topological invariant**
  (winding/linking; the specific one set by T0's rest structure), and it is an
  **integer**: discreteness is *forced by topology*, not installed by a tuned
  threshold. This meets the proposal's first could-fail test **conceptually**: if
  discreteness turns out to require a fitted threshold, the mapping fails here and
  we say so.
- **Correlated.** Closure (Op D) ↔ birth of identity ↔ the first nonzero integer
  invariant ↔ "primary topology / vorton" (Ch. 17); T0's enabling rest-state ↔
  the skin holding a self-locked, returned pucker. The invariant that appears is
  the seed of everything downstream (its twist part → magnetism, its writhe/route
  part → electricity, at T3).
- **Open.** *Which* invariant (2D winding vs 3D linking) — inherited directly from
  T0's open question. Containment observation: **the choice made at T0 constrains
  T2**, which constrains whether Lk = Tw + Wr (needs a ribbon/3D structure) is
  available downstream. This is the information → containment engine visibly at
  work: fixing the rest state tightens everything after it.
- **Falsifier.** Discreteness requires calibration; or a closed structure carries
  no conserved invariant at all.

## 7. What this phase is **not**

Not numeric, not fitted, not a physics claim. The scaffold ends when the ledger
is tight enough to justify *one* concrete instantiation — a Hopfion field
(background-assumption stripped) computing the T2 linking invariant parameter-free
— which is the first place the conceptual scaffold touches computation. Until
then: think laterally, strip assumptions, contain the space.
