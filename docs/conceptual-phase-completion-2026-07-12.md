## 1. What this phase was

**vorton** (primary topology) is the sole structural primitive, and atoms,
light, electricity and magnetism are its downstream expressions — E&M as
complementary modes of one substrate.

**Method (D5):** conceptual, not numeric. Strip buried assumptions from working
mathematics, keep only **background-free, coherence-native** patterns, and
**correlate** patterns across independent models. Progress measured as
**containment** (how tightly the admissible pattern-space is constrained by
robust, un-calibrated constraints), never as fit. Invariants over magnitudes.
**No fitted parameters, ever.** Every rung carries a pre-registered falsifier.

## 3. What was achieved

From the substrate's grain, *without fitting*, the **qualitative architecture of
matter** falls out:

- **Fermionic matter with spin-½** — the same Z₂ gives spin, statistics, and the
  charge shift (one θ=π, three results).
- **Quantised electric charge** — forced by the existence of the magnetic (π₂)
  sector (Dirac) and the θ=π fermion point (Witten); coupling-independent.
- **Electromagnetism, unified and half-derived** — one emergent bivector `F`,
  with `E`/`B` as its dynamic/held parts (twist/writhe), and the homogeneous
  Maxwell equations automatic (`dF = 0`). **No magnetic monopole** and
  **induction** are theorems here, not inputs.
- **The structure of chemistry** — Fermi exclusion → shells → periodic
  organisation, as a consequence of the derived spin-½.
- **A discrete species lattice** and **discrete spectra** (structural
  discreteness).

## 4. What is **NOT** claimed (the honest ceiling)

**This is a coherent, could-fail-tested *structural lens*, not validated
physics.** Specifically:

- **No number has been reproduced.** Everything is structure, sign, discreteness,
  and quantisation. Every magnitude — α, masses, binding energies, mass hierarchy,
  spectral line positions — is a **coupling/dynamical input, parked and forbidden
  from being fitted.**
- **The substrate is chosen, not proven.** The nematic RP²/3D grain is motivated
  (spin-½, magnetic geometry, background-independence) but unproven; its falsifier
  is explicit (if the vorton's 2π rotation were trivial in π₁(Q), no fermions).
- **Instantiation-level conditionals remain** — e.g. the microscopic generation of
  θ=π, and the explicit existence of the emergent magnetic sector.
- **Two confidence axes, kept strictly separate:** internal coherence / containment
  is **high and earned**; empirical correctness is **low and capped.** Tightening
  containment is *not* confirming physics. Conflating the two is exactly the
  original project's error (D1).

## 5. The discipline that held

No rung used a fitted parameter. Each named its falsifier in advance; none was
triggered. Where topology stopped (magnitudes, couplings, energetics), that
boundary was stated rather than papered over with a calibration — the precise
failure mode (D1, D4) this re-anchoring was built to avoid. Two self-corrections
were made in the open when checking revealed them ("charge mod 2" → sign-gauging;
"H = tQ²" → `H = nm` + linking).
