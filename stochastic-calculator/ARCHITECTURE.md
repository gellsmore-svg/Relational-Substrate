# Architecture and emergence contract

## Module boundaries

```text
rs_calc/numbers.py       boundary encoding and observation
rs_calc/engine.py        signed relations, stochastic events, faults, normalization
rs_calc/operations.py    population addition, cancellation, product and partition
rs_calc/radix.py         separate decimal bundle / T-C-R / borrow experiment
rs_calc/calculator.py    AST orchestration with relational intermediate values
rs_calc/cli.py           REPL, one-shot calculator and research commands
rs_calc/verification.py  independent conventional oracle and source audit
rs_calc/metrics.py       statistical measuring instruments
rs_calc/foundation.py    initial SC-001 experiment
rs_calc/experiments.py   declared trials and independent per-seed workers
rs_calc/provenance.py    manifests, fingerprints and historical source archives
rs_calc/replay.py        seeded replay against recorded trajectories
rs_calc/analyze.py       offline statistics and exportable figures
configs/                hypotheses, variables and criteria fixed before trials
tests/                  software regression tests, separate from research trials
research/               notebooks, raw datasets, measured results and figures
```

Dependency direction is deliberate: the mechanism does not import the oracle,
metrics or experiment runner. The harness calls both the mechanism and the
independent oracle, then compares their outputs. Statistical quantities and targets
cannot flow back through that interface into a calculation.

## Scope

This is an abstract computational model at the descriptive/implementation level,
not a simulation of T0 and not a claim that its graph edges are physical substrate
objects. It follows the repository's distinction between internal consistency and
empirical correctness. All results are conditional on explicitly chosen rules.

## Candidate representations, declared before SC-001

| Candidate | Identity | Assumptions and RS fit | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oriented relation population | Signed excess of positive over negative edges, modulo rewiring and neutral-pair insertion/removal | Explicit primitive multiplicity and polarity; topology can vary; transparent conservation proof; no metric or target needed | Select as minimal baseline |
| Connected-component or cycle topology | Component count / cycle rank | More topology-dependent; destructive edge noise changes identity; connectivity oracle and more bookkeeping | Defer, useful adversarial successor |
| Redundant constraint network | Agreement among overlapping witnesses | Can correct some erasures; original identity lives in redundant constraints; risks hiding a template | Compare only after conservation baseline |
| Attractor / energy network | Basin of a learned or imposed target | Requires a target/energy definition; easily pre-solves the task; ambiguous basin readout | Reject for initial apparatus |

Selection does NOT establish relations-first ontology: a signed multiset can be
redescribed as particles. Endpoint relations and their rewrite histories are real
model state, but multiplicity and polarity are explicitly supplied primitives.

## What counts

An operand is boundary-encoded into relations. Only relations, configuration and
a seeded PRNG enter the rewrite engine. A result may be observed only from the
output configuration. The engine may select edges, change incidence, cancel
opposite polarity, form products of relations, and partition through matching.
It may use ordinary arithmetic for IDs, indices, resource budgets and metrics.
It must not apply operand-level numeric arithmetic, lookup answers, call `eval`,
or receive an expected output, target number, or answer-dependent energy.

Boundary encoding (integer to population) and observation (population to decimal)
are conventional, openly admitted transducers. They do not evaluate expressions.
Host arithmetic for expected outcomes belongs only in `verification.py`, tests,
and offline analysis. Chaining must pass relational results, not decode/re-encode
intermediates. A source audit and independent operand grid test enforce this split.

Arithmetic rules themselves are designed. Therefore the defensible claim is
*schedule-independent arithmetic generated by designed stochastic rewrite rules*,
not discovery of arithmetic ex nihilo. A deterministic scheduler is a useful
comparator: stochasticity may add no computational power.

That comparator tests the role of scheduling, not the legitimacy of general
constraints. The programme does not require randomness to add computational power.
Designed constraints restricting stochastic trajectories are its intended subject.

## Fixed-proposal extension

`proposal_kernel.py` contains a separate answer-blind transition kernel; it imports
no numeric boundary or measuring module. `constraint_lab.py` owns encoding,
observation, fixed-horizon trials and replay. `analyze_constraints.py` owns statistics
and plots. This preserves historical mechanism/replay behavior while testing a
broader proposal grammar. Full specification and SC-023/025 evidence are in
[the constraint notebook](research/CONSTRAINT_LAB.md).

## First model

A relation has two region endpoints, polarity, and a source/output membership.
IDs name instances for traceability; they carry no numerical weight. Positive and
negative signs describe opposite orientations of an additive charge. Zero is the
empty normal form; arbitrarily many neutral pairs also instantiate zero before
normalization. An edge can be replaced by a new edge with different incidence
while retaining polarity. Opposite edges annihilate. Identity is the equivalence
class generated by incidence changes and neutral-pair creation/cancellation.

Global normalization randomly selects routing, cancellation, or rewiring events.
Local normalization restricts cancellation to a shared region and uses random
nearest-neighbour handoffs on a fixed ring. A global observer detects termination;
this is not autonomous distributed termination. No transition reads the target.
The local ring and polarity are imposed infrastructure, not spontaneously formed.

SC-001 must distinguish invariant-preserving disturbances from charge destruction.
Conservation alone predicts preservation under the former and cannot reconstruct
the latter without additional retained information. Calling both 'self repair'
would be a category error.

## Predeclaration

`configs/foundation.json` fixes the identity tests before observation. This is
algorithmic verification, not a material-property predictor: the repository's
physical-descriptor scoring gate is inapplicable (its registry contains material
descriptors). The dedicated runner records its configuration/hash before trials,
uses conventional arithmetic only as an independent comparator, and hashes all
mechanism sources. Later experiments must preserve their original manifest.

## Passive observers and recurrent dynamics

SC-026/027 use `dynamics_lab.py` for passive readouts, checkpoint measurements,
censored episodes and replay. `recurrent_kernel.py` wraps the existing proposal
kernel with a state-independent neutral-birth admission rule, retaining exact
conservation but allowing growth and readiness reversals. No expected value enters
either transition function. `defect_prediction.py` is an independent analytic
instrument; `analyze_dynamics.py` compares its predictions with raw trajectories.

Runs now archive the pre-execution protocol text and its hash alongside source
and configuration. Multiple checkpoints and simultaneous readouts are observations
of one trajectory, not independent runs. The default calculator is unaffected.
