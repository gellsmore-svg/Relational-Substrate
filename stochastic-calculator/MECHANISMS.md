# Mechanisms, constraints, and emergence audit

## Number formation

Write a population as oriented incidence records `(sign, source, target, readiness)`.
The observer's invariant is `Q = count(positive) - count(negative)`. Counts and
subtraction in this mathematical definition are the proof/observer, not an engine
target. Equivalence is generated by endpoint replacement, neutral-pair insertion,
and opposite-pair cancellation. All these rewrites preserve Q. A normalized state
has one sign and all records at the output boundary; its magnitude is its length.

This openly assumes discrete multiplicity and an orientation symmetry. It does not
derive numbers from an unstructured substrate. A graph-free multiset is a sufficient
description of global arithmetic; locality is the experiment in which endpoints
actually constrain admissibility. Interchanging indistinguishable records has no
observable effect. Structural hashes quotient out IDs and list order, but not
arbitrary permutations of the named background regions.

## Stochastic law

The PRNG uniformly chooses among enabled event TYPES, then among eligible records.
It is not Gillespie mass-action kinetics. Probabilities and transition counts have
no physical units. The default seed is randomly obtained at the CLI boundary;
every calculation reports it in verbose/JSON mode. A supplied seed fixes the entire
trajectory with the same source and Python version.

Unready records can hand off to output; any record can change incidence; opposite
signs can cancel. A handoff destroys the old record and instantiates a fresh one.
Ready records of mixed sign continue to evolve until cancellation finishes. Thus
the output is obtained through rewrites, not a cardinality calculation returned
before stochastic work begins. Some degenerate cases, including empty zero, have
no nontrivial stochastic trajectory. We report that rather than injecting noise.

In global mode, cancellation can connect any opposite records. In local mode it
requires a shared target region; handoff/rewiring moves to a ring neighbour. The
simulator enumerates eligible events and observes completion globally. A source
interpreter with a global event list is not an autonomous decentralized machine.

For the noiseless unary process, `V = unready_count + min(positive_count, negative_count)`
is a nonnegative progress function. Cancellation and handoff decrease V; incidence
rewrites preserve it. This proves invariant-preserving normalization when progress
events finish. Random scheduling gives almost-sure eventual progress on these
finite connected examples, but there is no finite worst-case random convergence
bound. Budgets produce censored failures, never a silently accepted partial answer.

## Arithmetic

| Operation | Generating mechanism | Load-bearing constraint | Explicit assumption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Addition | Reopen and redistribute the two relational populations | No creation/loss except neutral cancellation | Combining input populations supplies additive conservation |
| Subtraction | Reverse the right orientation, then redistribute/cancel | Opposite pairs cancel together | Chosen sign symmetry |
| Multiplication | Each ordered operand-relation pair emits one result relation | Pair obligation consumed exactly once; output sign is polarity equality | Global Cartesian construction and unbounded pair identities |
| Repeated comparator | One replica of right relations per left relation, normalize each row | One replica per anchor | Explicit repeated-addition procedure |
| Division | Stochastically bind distinct dividend relations to a copy of divisor slots; commit one quotient relation only after every slot is occupied | Injective complete matching; partial final matching becomes remainder | Global empty-slot / availability detection and sequential group commits |
| Decimal carry | Consume ten like-oriented relations; create a relation over the ten relations; replace its gate instance; instantiate one next-column relation | Bundle conservation, phase compatibility, consume once | Radix ten and ordered adjacent columns imposed |
| Decimal borrow | Split a higher-column relation into ten fresh next-lower relations, then cancel | Weighted conservation | Global sign/column conflict discovery chooses useful splits |

Division truncates toward zero. Its remainder has the dividend's sign, satisfies
`a = b*q + r`, and has magnitude smaller than the divisor. `/` retains both outputs;
`//` explicitly selects the truncating quotient, `%` selects the remainder. This
`//` convention differs from Python floor division for negative operands. Implicit
chaining of a nonzero remainder is rejected; use `(13//3)+1` or `(13%3)+1`.

Chaining passes populations through an AST interpreter; no intermediate result is
decoded to an integer for computation. Syntax precedence comes from Python's AST,
but `eval`, compiled expressions, and arbitrary calls are never used.

The general calculator uses unary populations. The `carry` command is a separate
decimal addition/subtraction apparatus, not a claim that unary `37+48` uses a carry.
Its digit readout counts fewer than ten surviving relations per column and joins
decimal characters. Its core does not calculate `a+b`, modulo, division, or a carry
bit. Borrow is explicitly programmed refinement, not a discovered carrier law.

## Higher-order structure and T-C-R

The product obligations relate two operand relations. Division relates a family
of slot/unit bindings to a commit. A decimal Gate contains a tuple of ten relation
references, whose joint admissibility authorizes a higher-column receiver. These
are operational relations over relations, implemented by ordinary data structures.

At transmit, members cease to be active at the digit boundary. At carry, the gate
instance is replaced. At receive, a fresh relation appears in the next column.
The certificate's information persists, though the same entity does not travel.
This demonstrates re-instantiation in a model; it does not prove information moves
without a carrier, or that reference records themselves are ontologically secondary.

No autonomous clock or stochastic transmission-delay model is implemented: event
ordering supplies timing variation, and simple one-gate carries always use three
phases. A full comparison of persistent transport versus local replicated boundary
state, with lost messages and asynchronous clocks, remains a follow-up.

## Constraint and fault interpretation

`strength` is precisely the probability of rejecting an attempted destructive
transition. `fault_rate` is the probability of proposing deletion before an active
unary step, or a receive replay in the radix model. These are different fault
channels, explicitly separated in the experiments. Strength is not a fitted RS
coherence score or emergent attractor depth. Full enforcement excludes that fault
channel by construction. Structural convergence can still give a wrong answer
after an admitted fault; the external oracle measures the distinction.

SC-014 removes only bundle replay exclusion, retaining the partition and phase
rules. Calling it an ablation of *all* higher-order structure would be false.
With no replay faults both variants are exact. Under faults the benefit is
conventional exactly-once enforcement; a first-order consumed marker could implement
the same abstract rule. Ontological superiority of higher-order relations is untested.

For positive `3+4`, let `d = fault_rate*(1-strength)`. Correctness requires surviving
until seven handoffs. Handoff probability is 1/2 at each active step. The geometric
survival factor per handoff is `(1-d)/(1+d)`, so predicted accuracy is its seventh
power. This no-fit conventional comparator was derived after SC-006 and locked
before SC-022 at new noise rates. Its success would defeat a special threshold or
deepening-well explanation for this curve.

## Audit conclusions

- No answer-valued target, answer lookup, host operand arithmetic, or numeric
  objective enters `operations.py`; the AST audit flags arithmetic operators there.
- Host arithmetic remains in input/output transducers, IDs, indices, scheduling,
  memory limits, seeds, the independent oracle, and analysis. This is disclosed,
  not a claim of eliminating mathematics from the implementation.
- Addition and subtraction are invariant resolutions. Multiplication and division
  depend on deliberately constructed global obligations and matching machinery.
- Radix ten, orientation, event types, conservation, exclusions, and observation
  maps are programmed. Exact arithmetic is not spontaneously invented by noise.
- The parser and completion observer provide central orchestration. Local
  cancellation is tested; fully distributed arithmetic and termination are not.
- Finite-state population-protocol impossibility results do not apply directly to
  this dynamically growing, labelled, globally orchestrated apparatus.
- Code auditing is a check, not a formal proof of noninterference. Tests include
  an observer-blocked relational addition and an independent signed operand grid.

## Engineering cost

Unary memory grows with magnitude; product obligations grow with the Cartesian
pair count. The current engine scans lists and hashes structural traces, so wall
time grows faster than the near-linear addition transition count. The default
20,000-relation and 100,000-transition limits are deliberate limits, not claims of
arbitrary precision at practical speed. Decimal carry is much more compact but is
currently a separate additive experiment. Parallel trials never share PRNG state;
worker count changes wall time, not a trial's transition semantics.
