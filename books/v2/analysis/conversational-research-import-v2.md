## 1. The 502-series: contextual identity, provenance and reconstruction

### Positive conceptual conclusions

- Identity is better understood as relational identity under transformation than as object identity.
- A state can be equivalent under one projection and distinguishable by a later interaction (contextual equivalence).
- Persistence can be re-instantiation within an equivalence class.
- Provenance (past distinctions surviving in present relational structure) matters where it remains consequential.
- A distinction can safely be compressed away only if no reachable later operation can consume it.
- A prior path can be encoded in present relational structure without a separate memory substance.

### Negative and conditional findings

Author-supplied conversational research indicates that:

- integrated runtimes could produce persistent differentiated lineages but often tended toward one dominant regime;
- reconstruction experiments could restore recursive organisation without demonstrating that the *same identity* had been restored;
- identity-specific recovery sometimes depended on contextual scaffolding;
- scrambled or context-altered cases could regenerate recursive, functionally useful structure without recovering the original lineage;
- recursive persistence or repair is therefore not identical to identity-specific recovery;
- no universal endogenous identity mechanism was established;
- lineage and carrier identity remained conditional and incompletely resolved.

### Four restoration concepts that must not be conflated

| Concept | Meaning | Example of the gap |
|---|---|---|
| Functional recovery | the system again performs the relevant operation | a replacement part that works |
| Structural or recursive recovery | the system again shows the relevant organisation or recursive pattern | regenerated structure after scrambling |
| Equivalence-class recovery | the restored state falls in the same relevant equivalence class | indistinguishable for every reachable future interaction of the relevant kind |
| Lineage or identity-specific recovery | the same identity in the provenance-sensitive sense | not demonstrated in every reconstructed case |

**Core caution:** reconstruction is not automatically restoration of identity. In the book this is reflected in Chapter 6 ("Preserving is not repairing") and Appendix B. It also supports the theological guard in Chapter 14 that resurrection is God's act, not natural reconstruction. That guard rests on Scripture; the research only shows why a natural mechanism would not suffice.

## 2. Stochastic rings and local–global coordination

### Positive conceptual conclusions

- Similar local stochastic characteristics do not uniquely determine global behaviour.
- What matters is consequence-aligned, state-dependent coordination (which events are coupled, in which direction, under which conditions, what is preserved, what changes downstream), not correlation as such.
- Mean closure can hold while fluctuation closure stays open.

### Calibration added in v2.2

Ordinary aggregation of independent or weakly dependent events already produces stable means. That is mainstream probability and is not credited to RS. Coordination matters for fluctuation structure, exact conservation, trajectory behaviour, response to perturbation and accessibility of future states. The layered result is that mean closure is not fluctuation closure, and fluctuation closure is not the full relational closure of the whole.

### Repository support for adjacent points

Calculator SC-013 (ring locality: exact but costlier) and SC-017 (designed covariance).

### Still open

A formal ring experiment comparing equal-marginal processes under independent, shared and state-dependent coupling, reporting means and higher moments.

## 3. Navier–Stokes and effective law

The conclusions are philosophical and mainstream in their science: deterministic continuum equations are effective descriptions, and deterministic mathematics at one scale does not imply determinism at every scale. The RS question begins after accepting the mainstream science: *which relational conditions make stable effective law possible?* **No RS derivation exists.**

## 4. Positive Grassmannian / amplituhedron: projection and hidden structure

The conclusion is conceptual: equivalence under a chosen projection does not imply dynamical equivalence, and hidden distinctions are irrelevant only where no reachable future process can consume them. In the book the specialist terms appear only in Appendix B, and the main text teaches the general lesson.
