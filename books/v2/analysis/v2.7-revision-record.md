## Deviations from the Phase A plan

- **Order of work.** Editorial cuts were made chapter by chapter, immediately before each chapter's additions, not in one pass across the book first. "Delete before elaborating" held within each chapter, and each chapter was read once instead of twice.
- **Shorter drafts.** Chapters 12–16 were drafted more tightly than the plan's prose because of the reading budget.
- **Q3(b).** The author chose the stronger grounding objection. The Chapter 3 paragraph states it as internally contradictory under the book's grounding requirement, and expressly not as a claim about mathematical infinities.
- **Q6(b).** Making the *that* of B a premise excluded A and C at premise level. Chapter 3, Chapter 9 and App E.9 were reworded accordingly, beyond the plan's §4.7.
- **Q11.** The author chose post-Fall animal mortality. The resulting fossil-record interface was put to the author (option 1: state, name the conflict, do not resolve).
