## Provenance warning

The briefs name the unabridged 31 October conversations and the preceding determinism/ABCD conversation as the primary evidence. **Neither was supplied.** The sources available were the coherence audit, the score workbook, the integration brief with its §7–§8 supplement, and Addendum 2 (all in `../prompts/v2.7-sources/`). All are assistant-prepared. Consequently:

- no unit has a transcript locator; the locator column cites the audit or the brief section instead;
- "author position" means *the briefs say the author holds this*, not that the wording was checked against the author's own words;
- the same rule applied in `conversational-research-import-v2.md`: cite as author-supplied conversational research until the source is available.

Speaker and status codes: **AX** author position attested in the briefs; **AC** author correction of an assistant framing; **TX** Scripture text; **AO** audit only; speaker not identifiable; **AS** analyst challenge or open question.

Actions: `retain` (already in the book, unchanged), `clarify` (a sentence or wording), `add` (new text), `cross-reference`, `research` (App E open question, not chapter text), `omit`, `ask-author`. Book locations are `ChN Lnn` line numbers in `manuscript/` at revision 2.6 (7dd10a8).
