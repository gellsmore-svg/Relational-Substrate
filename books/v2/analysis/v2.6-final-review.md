# Revision 2.6: final adversarial review

Deliverable 7 of the brief (Part 14). Each review below records what was examined, what was wrong, what was changed, and what remains unresolved. The corrections are in the manuscript and in `v2.6-ontological-revision-record.md` §6.

## Review A: ontological integrity

*Have materiality, physical reality, mathematical representation, identity and fundamentality stayed distinct?*

- **Examined.** The definition's statements in Ch 3, Ch 6, Appendices C and E; the four-way split of carrier, activity, organisational identity and description; the treatment of fields and phonons.
- **Found and corrected.**
  - **A-1.** Ch 6 called the stone "a material individual made of material individuals". That contradicted the chapter's own regime point, because binding electrons are not individuated. It now says the stone is composed *partly* of material individuals.
  - **A-2.** Ch 6's restatement of the definition dropped "mechanically". It was restored, so the definition reads the same everywhere.
  - **A-3.** "Return materially the same" (Ch 2) used *material* in its loose ordinary sense; changed to "substantially".
- **Holds.** "Physical" and "material" are distinct throughout. Fields are not called material in any sentence. The phonon case is used only to show that formalism does not decide ontology.
- **Unresolved.** Whether a single photon would satisfy the definition is not argued (record §10).

## Review B: T-C-R integrity

*Is T-C-R still minimal? Are the new identity functions roles rather than substances?*

- **Examined.** Ch 7 against E.4's "not presupposed" list; the organiser and coherer definitions; Ch 8's use of them.
- **Found and corrected.** **B-1.** The radio example and the organiser/coherer roles brought modulation and demodulation, which are encoding and decoding, into the chapter right after the grammar. E.4 says T-C-R does not presuppose them. Ch 7 now says that neither role belongs to the minimal grammar, that a single distinction can be transmitted and received with no organising or cohering, and that modulation is a particular case.
- **Holds.** Both roles are defined functionally ("because of what they do"), are said not to be substances or fields by definition, and are not located solely in the receiver: the carrier's format does cohering work (FM). Their irreducibility is left open.
- **Unresolved.** Whether the roles are specialisations of T and R, or something more basic, is open by design.

## Review C: qualitative probability

*Are comparisons defensible without invented precision, and are their assumptions preserved?*

- **Examined.** Ch 2's three judgments and classification; Ch 3's interoperability comparison; E.9's assessment bullets.
- **Found and corrected.** **C-1.** The global-coherence principle, quoted from the brief, said "logically admissible". In this book *admissible* means physically possible, and the metrics showed the word now appearing before Ch 4 defines it. Changed to "logically possible", which also matches the three-judgment vocabulary.
- **Holds.** Every comparison in Ch 3 is marked conditional, with its premise ("*if* each kind's capacities were specified independently"). No number or score appears anywhere. The converse error (possibility carrying a number; complexity implying improbability) is named.
- **Unresolved.** The B-over-C ordering remains conditional on B's unpaid debt. The book says so.

## Review D: comparative ontology

*Are A, B and C represented fairly? Are burdens assessed? Are overclaims corrected?*

- **Examined.** Ch 3 "Three propositions"; E.9 table and bullets.
- **Found and corrected.**
  - **D-1.** A's first route (intrinsically partner-directed capacities) was said to "move toward B". That route is A's own wording, so it was corrected to "faithful to A, but narrows the gap with B".
  - **D-2.** C was said to have "only moved the coincidence". C explains matching by its common order, as B does, so this now reads "part of the coincidence", with the residue named.
  - **D-3.** The draft's emphasis followed the brief in treating composite interoperability as strong evidence. Composition explains it under every ontology, so the argument was moved to the base grammar.
  - **D-4.** B is presented as a family that includes field realism, so physics-based considerations are not credited to RS.
- **Holds.** No impossibility claim is made against A or C. The verdict is graded as robust, conditional or unresolved.
- **Unresolved.** RS versus field realism within the B family. B versus C.

## Review E: biblical coherence

*Are creation, sustenance, creaturely limits, moral agency and spiritual reality handled consistently? Are theological claims kept apart from mechanisms?*

- **Examined.** Ch 2 (creaturely knowledge), Ch 3 (design and the propositions), Ch 5 (four confusions), Ch 8 (receiver participation, dodo, soul, persons), Ch 16 additions.
- **Checks made.** Ch 8's statement that Ch 15 rejects resurrection as reconstruction was verified against Ch 15's text ("it is not the reconstruction of an indistinguishable replacement"). Receiver participation is explicitly not idealism: created things are real before anything receives them (Ch 1). The music analogy carries an explicit soul guard. Design is said to be compatible with all three propositions and not decisive. No Scripture is said to specify a mechanism, and no new Scripture citations were introduced.
- **Found and corrected.** Nothing substantive. The first draft of the Ch 8 sentence on persons attributed "a person is not the information that survives about them" to Ch 15. It now separates the attribution (resurrection is not reconstruction) from the book's own sentence.
- **Unresolved.** None identified. Chapters 10–15 were not altered except for chapter numbers.

## Review F: recursive emergence

*Is the step from distinction to higher-order identity explained, the interface clear, and model kept apart from ontological transition?*

- **Examined.** Ch 8's grammar, tests, interface and pier sections; E.9.
- **Found and corrected.** **F-1.** "Distinction → relationship" could be read as putting something before relation, against Ch 3's thesis. Ch 8 and E.9 now state that the grammar is an order of articulation, not of priority in being. A distinction is already relational, and at the lowest order the first two stages may be given together.
- **Holds.** The interface alternatives (fifth stage, interface, first stage of the next order, boundary condition) are listed and left open. The working reading (upward as distinction, downward as constraint) is labelled a reading. Haken and music are both said to show emergence *within* matter only. Its ancestry in the first edition's stack is stated.
- **Unresolved.** Whether the three tests are sufficient, as well as necessary, for a new order.

## Review G: reader coherence

*Can a thoughtful non-specialist follow it? Do definitions stay stable? Are conclusions visible?*

- **Examined.** Concept load per chapter; definition stability; bolded conclusions; metrics.
- **Found and corrected.**
  - **G-1.** Ch 8 introduced about ten concepts. The geometry paragraph was moved to Appendix B and E.5.
  - **G-2.** The definition was stabilised (A-2).
  - **G-3.** The term collision was fixed (C-1).
  - **G-4.** Three musical-precision fixes were made for a specialist reader: the Prelude's opening is arpeggiated, not "rising"; "particle of sound" was removed; and the wolf tone is one *result* of mode coupling.
- **Holds.** Every new section begins with a concrete case (stone, clap, radio, Bach, dodo, fence, private function) before its term. Key conclusions are bolded once, where they are stated: the description-of-consequences rule, the fence principle, the global-coherence principle, the carrier principle, the information principle and the central question. The prerequisite-order violations are identical to v2.5's.
- **Unresolved.** The main path is 2.9% over its budget. Chapter 6 (about 4,400 words by the metrics script) is now the longest physical chapter. A future pass could split its materiality material into its own chapter if readers find it heavy.

## Unresolved contradictions

None found that the book does not already flag. Three tensions are recorded rather than resolved:

1. The book prefers B while admitting that B's central mechanism is undemonstrated. The tension is stated as a debt in Ch 3, Ch 9 and E.9.
2. Physics-based considerations favour the B family, while the book's thesis is a specific member of it.
3. The definition of *material* is anchored in the manifest world, while the substrate's spatiality is open. The definition deliberately does not reach below manifest space.

## Most promising research directions

1. **Causal states of the laboratory's visible processes** (Generations 3 and 8H–8P). A direct, already-specified way to measure "history as present state" and the cost of hiding relations.
2. **Endogenous localised persistence.** Rule families in which persistent, individuated, interacting patterns arise without being designed in, judged against matched nulls. This bears directly on B's debt.
3. **Order-parameter reduction** in any generation, as the first model evidence of downward constraint.
4. **Divisibility of coarse-grained processes** and its relation to registration events (Barandes's division events), using the Generation 3 set-up.
5. **Comparative judgments as partial orders** in the research-synthesis coherence register, replacing any implicit scoring.
6. **A clearer statement of what would discharge B's debt**: the minimal demonstration that would move B's status from "working hypothesis with an unpaid debt" toward "supported".
