# Epistemic register

This register is the control against prose silently promoting a hypothesis to a fact. Every load-bearing claim in the second edition is listed with its category, its support, the considerations against it, a confidence level, where it appears in the book, its research source and its status.

**Categories:**

- scriptural
- observational
- experimental-RS
- mathematical
- scientific-consensus
- scientifically-contested
- philosophical
- ontological
- theological-inference
- RS-working-hypothesis
- speculative

**Confidence** expresses how strongly the book may assert the claim. It is not the probability that the claim is true.

- **H**: assert plainly.
- **M**: assert with stated grounds.
- **L**: offer as hypothesis.
- **S**: name as speculation.
