## 6. Red-team findings, including corrections to the brief

These were challenged before final prose (brief Part 11.2). Several correct or qualify the brief.

1. **Composite interoperability is weaker than presented.** In any compositional ontology new composites inherit their parts' compatibility, so "no separately specified rules for new combinations" is explained under A as well. The real burden is the base grammar. The book argues from the base (charge equality) and says so (Ch 3; E.9).
2. **B is a family.** A realist reading of quantum fields that does not call fields material is a non-material foundation. The physics-based considerations (particle creation, indistinguishability) favour the family, not RS specifically. RS's relational claim needs its own argument. This is a substantive qualification of the brief's Part 16 framing.
3. **A's first route is faithful to A.** Partner-directed intrinsic capacities are what A says ("capacities belong intrinsically"). The first draft said this "moves toward B"; corrected to "narrows the gap with B" (Review D).
4. **C explains matching as B does.** The first draft said C "only moves the coincidence"; corrected to "part of the coincidence", the residue being why independently fundamental matter fits the order (Review D).
5. **Organisational individuals satisfy the letter of the definition.** Solitary waves and skyrmions are persistent, individuated and relocatable. Rather than widen the definition or deny the cases, a clarifying rule classes them as material organisation with entity-like individuality. This also gives the central question its sharpest observable analogue.
6. **Under B, handling cannot be "stuff travels".** If matter is itself re-established form, relocating a stone is at depth pattern re-instantiation (as the first edition said of motion). The handling/perturbation distinction is therefore drawn by preserved identity, which is how the definition is worded (Ch 6).
7. **Regime dependence.** Single trapped electrons are individuated; conduction electrons are not. Materiality belongs first to configurations, then to kinds. A consequence found in review: the stone's binding electrons are not individuated, so a material individual can be composed partly of non-individuated constituents (Ch 6, corrected from "made of material individuals").
8. **Photons are a borderline case left open.** Pair production is described as matter arising from radiation, following physics' own matter/radiation distinction. Whether a single photon held in a cavity would meet the definition is not argued in the book. It is recorded here as an unresolved edge (§10).
9. **The charge datum is indirect.** The ≈10⁻²¹ bound (Bressi et al. 2011) assumes charge conservation in neutron decay. Appendix D says so.
10. **B's structural advantage is not a derivation.** B locates where an explanation of matched charges would lie; it does not supply one. The book states B's debt as "the largest open debt in this book" and lists it in Ch 9's counter-evidence.
11. **Receiver participation needed non-conscious cases.** To avoid idealism, the book uses the rainbow and tuning forks as well as the ear, and guards that the source is real before reception (Ch 8; E.7).
12. **The equivalence criterion is not novel.** It is the causal-state construction of computational mechanics. Appendix D's "no direct precedent" list was qualified accordingly.
13. **The brief's Barandes critique could not be located as attributed.** The trajectory point is stated as following from Barandes's own text, and that is recorded.
14. **Two factual claims were dropped on verification.** That dodo painters depicted overfed captive birds, and that oscillons formed "molecules and polymers" in the 1996 paper. The book now says only what was verified.
15. **"Distinction before relationship" versus relation-first.** The grammar could be read as contradicting Ch 3. It is now stated as an order of articulation, not of priority in being (Ch 8; E.9).
16. **Organiser and coherer must not reintroduce encoding.** E.4 says T-C-R presupposes no encoding or decoding. The book now says the roles are not part of the minimal grammar, and that modulation is a particular case (Ch 7).
17. **Theological boundary checks.** Music across carriers: "a soul is not a melody played on a body". Dodo reconstruction: resurrection is not reconstruction (verified against Ch 15's own wording). Design: compatible with A, B and C, and not decisive. Chance: four categories separated.

## 8. Changes to the research possibility space

- **Higher-order identity becomes testable** in the project's models by three criteria: persistence beyond constituents against a matched null, consequence as a unit, and multiple realisability. The first is already a GT method (OP-18).
- **Causal states offer a direct measurement** of "history as present state" in the Constraint Laboratory's visible processes.
- **Divisibility of coarse-grained processes** connects the laboratory's Generation 3 result to Barandes's division events.
- **Order-parameter reductions** in laboratory generations would be the first model-level evidence of the downward role.
- **Endogenous redundancy and localised persistent patterns** are the adjacent "piers" most relevant to the emergence of material individuality. They must be pursued under OTR's ban on result bias.
- **B's debt** is now the explicit target: any finding that persistent, individuated, interacting organisation arises from simpler relations without being designed in would bear on it directly.
