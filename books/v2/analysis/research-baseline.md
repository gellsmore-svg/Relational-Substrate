# Part I — Current baseline

## I.1 The organising problem

A discipline can be locally exact and still leave globally consequential ontological questions unanswered. Physics predicts with extraordinary success. It does not thereby settle why its entities are mutually compatible, why one interaction grammar holds everywhere, why mathematically describable regularity exists at all, how life and personhood belong to the same order, or why moral consequence is not alien to the physical world. The book asks:

> What kind of created reality can make all the things we know (physical, living, personal, moral and revealed) belong coherently to one world?

Its answer is relation-first: created physical order is relational from its foundation. The proposal is judged by global coherence, not by compatibility with physics alone.

## I.2 The live argument (canonical order)

```text
global coherence          the standard: one created order must hold the physical, living, personal,
                          moral and revealed without silent category change or unexplained interfaces
→ relational primacy      things are constituted within relations; universal interoperability
                          (independently located things share one interaction grammar) is a datum
                          that every ontology must pay for
→ constrained actualisation   T0 names whatever conditions actualisation (ontology open); T1 is
                          actualised, history-bearing order; actualisation is stochastic (RS working
                          hypothesis); present history shapes what is currently reachable
→ consequential relation  T-C-R: distinction → preserved dependency → consequence; candidate
                          grammar of consequential manifestation, possibly recursive
→ persistence and identity   sameness of kind = relevant equivalence class judged by reachable futures;
                          provenance where consequential; lineage (individual continuity) distinct
→ stable manifestation    layered regularity: aggregation stabilises means; coordination shapes the
                          rest; effective laws describe scales; projection can hide what later matters
→ life → personhood → moral consequence → corruption → restoration
```

The arrows are an order of argument. They are not a stack of layers, a causal pipeline, or a claim that each item is the same kind of thing.

## I.3 Epistemic stance

**Global coherence is bidirectional.** RS must not contradict observations, measurements, successful calculations or well-tested models. Equally, the ontological assumptions conventionally carried by physics are tested for coherence. They have no exemption because they are familiar, published or taught. Six layers are kept apart:

1. what was observed;
2. what was measured;
3. the mathematical relation that predicts it;
4. the physical model that organises it;
5. an ontological interpretation of the model;
6. the worldview in which that interpretation feels natural.

Layers 1–3 constrain the ontology. Layers 4–6 are candidates, judged like RS.

**Global coherence** comprises:

- connectivity across domains;
- valid interfaces, explained rather than assumed;
- preserved distinctions, with no silent change of meaning in translation;
- propagation stability, so that a fix in one domain does not break another;
- cycle consistency, so that a concept returns from a tour of domains unchanged or with the change explained;
- category integrity, since connection is not reduction and analogy is not identity;
- epistemic compatibility, so that observation, mathematics, inference and revelation stay distinct and mutually accountable.

Coherence is not proof of truth. The book says so once, in Chapter 2, and does not repeat it as an apology. It is the right comparative discipline where empirical description underdetermines ontology.

**Prose rule.** A proposition is stated first, with its status, and qualified afterwards. Underclaiming is an epistemic failure as real as overclaiming.

## I.4 Current canonical definitions

**T0 (reopened in v2.4).**

- *Minimal commitment:* T0 names whatever is logically prior to, and conditions, actualisation: the constraints, potential, admissibility, organisation or relational conditions according to which a subsequent actual state can occur.
- *Open question:* what kind of thing that is. Live readings include:
  - an admissibility structure, "constrained possibility", which the books have used since the first edition;
  - a created potential order;
  - a generative constraint architecture;
  - the relational state of the substrate itself, viewed prior to a particular actualisation (the project's AMS research notes use T0 for "the continuous substrate domain", inferred rather than observed);
  - a formulation not yet found.
- "Possibility space" is a useful reading, not a realm, store or place.

**T1.** Actuality: actualised, history-bearing relational order. It is not a sequence of memoryless snapshots. Present structure carries provenance where future interactions can discriminate it.

- The T0–T1 relation is not a one-way pipeline. Total admissibility, whatever T0 ultimately fixes, is distinct from *currently reachable* admissibility, which depends on present state and provenance. Actuality participates in the conditions of its own next actualisation without thereby altering T0.
- No new notation is introduced for this.

**T-C-R (deepened in v2.4).** The grammar of consequential manifestation: the logical structure by which one distinction becomes consequential to another state.

- *Transmit:* a distinction in one relational condition is capable of constraining something other than itself.
- *Carry:* the dependency between source distinction and consequence preserves enough of the distinction for the consequence to depend on it. The carrier need not be a separate object; at the most primitive level the relation itself may be the carrier. Test: if distinctions A and B at the source can never make any difference to any consequence, the distinction has not been carried.
- *Receive:* another state becomes actual, or its admissible actualisation is constrained, because of the source distinction. Reception is consequence, not arrival.
- Nothing about travel, metric space, intermediate objects, encoding, dimensional reduction or a layer stack is presupposed.
- The source need not be consumed. Reception can be repeated, cumulative, saturating, redundant or destructive; exactly-once is a transactional subcase.
- **RS working hypothesis:** T-C-R is not a mechanism added to relation but the operational form of consequential relation itself, which would explain its recurrence from substrate to speech. A receiver at one stage can transmit at the next, so the grammar may be recursive and networked.
- **Working causal formulation:** a causal relation exists where a distinction in one relational condition modifies the admissible actualisation or consequential state of another through a distinction-preserving dependency. It can do this by excluding states, admitting them, weighting them, constraining correlations, preserving an invariant, or changing later accessibility. It was tested against the constraint laboratory (record v2.4 §6) and is held as a hypothesis.
- Carrier architecture in all models so far was designed. Endogenous emergence of carriers and lineages is an **open problem**.

**Manifestation.** Ordinary created order presenting itself to other things. The substrate and the manifest physical order may be:

- two descriptions of one relational fact;
- different resolutions of one order;
- related by an unknown mapping;
- related in a way not yet conceived.

The book does not say that something invisible is "transformed" into something visible. The commitment is only that a consequence-preserving relation holds between underlying distinction and manifest actuality. The T2 label stays retired. Conscious awareness belongs to the personal category and is not a substrate tier.

**Spatiality (open).** Three families remain live:

- *intrinsic*: substrate structure corresponds significantly to experienced space;
- *emergent*: extension arises from a non-spatial relational organisation;
- *proto-spatial*: adjacency, ordering or topology from which metric space emerges.

Scripture provokes questions about naive container-space. It does not supply a substrate geometry.

**Identity (physical).**

- *Sameness of kind or state* is continuity of the relevant equivalence class through admissible change, relative to what reachable futures can discriminate.
- *Individual continuity* requires lineage and history in addition. What carries it physically is open.
- Invariants certify equivalence in some systems.
- Provenance belongs to identity where consequential.
- Four restoration concepts are kept apart: functional, structural/recursive, equivalence-class, and lineage. Reconstruction is not automatically restoration of identity.
- Personal identity resembles physical identity but is not defined by it.

**Regularity.** Effective determinism is regularity stable to the precision observed. Mathematical determinism is a property of equations. Quantities of a whole may be stable in mean, concentrated, exactly conserved or still stochastic. "Deterministic" is never a universal label for the global level. Mathematics describes regularity without being the reality described, and a successful model does not uniquely settle what exists.

**Universal interoperability.** Independently located physical things affect one another, have compatible receptivity, obey the same interaction relations, have commensurable quantities and compose into stable structures. An ontology of self-contained objects must explain this common interface somewhere: common laws, fields, symmetries, forms, dispositions or generative history. A common relational ground explains it directly. This is an explanatory-coherence argument, not a probability calculation.

## I.6 Theological constraints

- Scripture first; KJV quotation. The Creator/creature distinction is absolute. The substrate, T0, T1, T-C-R, stochasticity, mathematics, information and physical order are created, and none functions as a disguised divine attribute.
- Four relations are distinguished: Scripture teaches X; RS is consistent with X; RS proposes a mechanism compatible with X; Scripture provokes a question RS explores. Biblical language does not manufacture physics, and Scripture is not decoration added after the physics.
- Covenant-neutral and denomination-neutral, including on freedom. Stochasticity is not a proof of free will.
- Fallibility is located in interpretation, never in Scripture or measurement as such.
- Personhood is deeper than embodiment. The anthropology of dust, breath and living soul is unified.
- Human death is not humanity's intended created end. There is no blanket claim about all pre-Fall biological death.
- Universal resurrection with judgment. Resurrection is God's act, not natural reconstruction.
- Corruption is parasitic and not ultimate. Evil was not necessary: a real command and a real possibility of disobedience preceded the Fall (Genesis 2–3), so moral freedom does not require actualised evil. How humanity would otherwise have come to know good and evil is speculation, not teaching.
- Redemption is restoration and consummation accomplished by God, not substrate repair.

## I.7 Thesis (one paragraph)

God creates a real world, and its physical order is relational from the foundation. Things are constituted within relations, not first made as isolated objects and then connected, which is why independently located things everywhere share one grammar of interaction. Actualisation is conditioned (T0 names those conditions, whose final ontology is open) and, on this book's working hypothesis, stochastic. What happens carries its history into the present and shapes what can happen next. A distinction becomes consequential to another state through a dependency that preserves it, and this grammar of transmission, carriage and reception may be the form of consequential relation itself. Things remain the same kind of thing by remaining within their relevant equivalence class; being the same individual is a further question of lineage. Regularity is layered, and mathematics describes it with extraordinary success without being the reality described. Because the material order is relational at base, life, personhood, responsibility, corruption and restoration are continuous in form with the world they arise in, yet relation across categories does not erase category: the material is not the spiritual, the substrate is not the soul, and creation is not God. The argument is one of global coherence, constrained by observation and governed by Scripture. It is stated clearly enough to be wrong.

### Constraint Laboratory status at v2.3.1

Repository branches `research/constraint-lab-v0.2` (head `0170189`) and `research/constraint-lab-v0.3` (head `e04e0d4`).

- **Generation 1** reported a cancelling reciprocal pair. Generation 1b reclassified that pair as one structural rule with stacked grades, a representational artifact. G1's committed records remain as history.
- **Generation 1b:**
  - genuine global cancellation in the N = 3 edge grammar uses structured triples (40);
  - exact kernels hide inside coarse families (up to 1,042 in one family);
  - observable equality is not kernel equality.
- **Generation 2** (occupation-count literal):
  - 654 new exact kernels, 189 new families and 30 new supports, all from count-gated prohibitions;
  - soft weights opened no supports;
  - 16 genuine cancellations as reciprocal count-literal pairs;
  - constructive dissolution not found.
- **There is no universal slogan** such as "cancellation requires triples".
- **Generation 3** (independent three-way relation, N = 3):
  - irreducible dynamic coupling arises when a triadic condition governs a pairwise action (2,940 of 7,385 sets);
  - hiding the triad makes the pairwise process non-Markov in 5,250 sets: apparent memory with H = 0;
  - pure pairwise rules produce neither effect;
  - no new pairwise supports and no global cancellations;
  - constructive reorganisation is not expressible at N = 3;
  - the laboratory's next justified step is an N = 4 probe, not H = 1.
- None of these results shows that nature uses the grammar. Negative results are treated as constraints on the programme.

### Constraint Laboratory, later generations (consulted for v2.4)

## III.1 Original v2.0 prompt-versus-evidence divergences (first rewrite brief §50)

These records are preserved as written at the v2.0 stage, each with a supersession note where later work changed the conclusion.

### D-1 The slogan "stochastic locally / constrained relationally / deterministic globally"

The slogan was replaced at v2.0 by *stochastic in path, constrained in admissibility, stable in invariant*, with "effective determinism" defined, because the calculator's exactness came from programmed conservation, not from noise.

> **Refined (v2.2):** stability is layered (mean, concentration, exact conservation, residual stochasticity). "Invariant" is generalised to "equivalence". See I.3, Regularity.

### D-2 T-C-R wording (v2.0)

The v2.0 formulation read the calculator protocol as the definition: transmit = "release … at a cost to the source configuration"; receive = "an admissible change of state elsewhere … and it happens once". The book grammar was `difference → release → constrained continuity → consequential reception`.

> **Superseded (v2.1):** source consumption and once-only reception were features of one arithmetic protocol, not of T-C-R. The current definition is in I.3.

### D-3 "Recursive relational admissibility plus recursive composability"

At v2.0 the 502-series was not located, so the phrase was not canonised and only verified admissibility and illustrated composition were used.

> **Superseded (v2.1, v2.2):** the 502 conclusions entered as author-supplied exploratory research, both positive and negative (`conversational-research-import-v2.md` §1). The phrase itself is still not canonised.

### D-4 Memory as persistent configuration

At v2.0 memory was treated as a working hypothesis, with directional order-effect support and calculator carried state. History depth H was then 0.

> **Updated (v2.2):** Generation 3 shows apparent memory arising from a hidden present relation at H = 0. Memory remains a working hypothesis at the level of physical generality.

### D-5 Navier–Stokes, spectral/Grassmannian and population research

At v2.0 none of this was located, and only mainstream fluid dynamics was used.

> **Superseded (v2.1):** the conclusions entered as author-supplied exploratory research. Still no RS Navier–Stokes derivation and no amplitude-physics claim.

### D-6 Teleology and T0

Adopted as a theological inference: purpose shapes possibility; possibility shapes actualisation; the substrate intends nothing.

> **Unchanged.**

### D-7 T0 naming collision

The July ladder uses T0–T7 for rungs; the books use T0 for constrained possibility.

> **Unchanged.** T2 was retired in v2.1.

> **Updated (v2.4):** a third usage was found. The AMS research notes use T0 for the continuous substrate domain. "Constrained possibility" is now one live reading of T0, not its definition (I.4).
