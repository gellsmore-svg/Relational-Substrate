## 1. Reader observations

These come from one human read of the finished v2.3.1 book.

- Chapters 9, 10 and 11 are excellent, among the strongest work in the project. Chapters 12 and 13 are very strong. Chapter 14 is strongly liked.
- The introduction and preface are comparatively weak. They do not give the reader the reason the book exists.
- Parts of the book, chiefly the early chapters, drift back into a conventional science textbook: tours of accepted physics, scale colour, entities reified before the ontology has earned them.
- Accumulated caveats bury propositions. The prose carries the history of its own peer review.
- Global coherence is framed mostly in one direction: RS must not contradict physics. The reverse audit, of the metaphysics conventionally carried by physics, is underdeveloped.
- T0 has been frozen as "constrained possibility". That is one reading, not a settled ontology.
- T-C-R is treated mainly through manifest physical examples. Its deeper role, as the grammar by which any distinction becomes consequential, is missing.
- Research history (programmes, calculator stages, earlier routes) is overrepresented in the reader narrative. The stochastic calculator has become a recurring mascot.
- "Researchers" implies a human team. There is one human researcher and author, working with extensive AI-assisted and computational exploration.
- There is no single place where the reader can find the book's current ontology.

## 2. Governing constraints for v2.4

1. **Global coherence is bidirectional.** Observation, measurement, successful mathematics and repeatable regularity constrain the ontology. Conventional interpretations, entity vocabularies and disciplinary habits do not gain evidential weight from familiarity or consensus. The six layers stay apart: observation, measurement, mathematical relation, physical model, ontological interpretation, worldview.
2. **Global coherence is defined strongly:** connectivity across domains, valid interfaces, preserved distinctions, propagation stability, cycle consistency, category integrity and epistemic compatibility. "Coherence is not proof of truth" is said once, clearly.
3. **State, then qualify.** A proposition comes first, with its status. Limits come after it, at the end of the paragraph or in a short status note, not woven through it.
4. **Science serves the argument.** It earns its place by supplying an observation any ontology must explain, a mathematical constraint, a case of underdetermination, or a concrete illustration of a relational principle.
5. **"Universe" is not a primitive.** Prefer creation, created order, physical order, world, cosmos, heavens and earth. Keep "universe" only for named proposals.
6. **One human author.** No research team. AI assistance is named accurately where provenance matters and is otherwise not a subject.
7. **T0 is reopened.** Its minimal commitment is that it names whatever conditions actualisation. Its ontology is open.
8. **T1 participates in its own conditions.** Total admissibility is distinguished from admissibility currently reachable given present state and provenance. No new permanent notation is introduced.
9. **T-C-R is the grammar of consequential manifestation:** distinction → preserved dependency → consequence. No travel, metric space, intermediate object, encoding, layer stack, exactly-once reception or source consumption is presupposed. The carrier may be the relation itself. The claim that consequential relation as such instantiates T-C-R is a stated hypothesis. A causal formulation is tested against the stochastic work before it is adopted.
10. **Substrate spatiality stays open:** intrinsic, emergent and proto-spatial families remain live. "Transformation", "projection", "mapping" and "passes through" are not used to commit to one.
11. **Universal interoperability is a datum.** Independently located things share one interaction grammar. Every ontology is charged for the machinery that explains this. The argument is explanatory, not a pseudo-probability.
12. **The calculator is illustration, not evidence.** One strong use stays. Its detail moves to Appendix B.
13. **Category integrity:** Creator/creation; structural analogy is not identity; information transfer is not consciousness; substrate persistence is not resurrection. Guards go where confusion is possible, not everywhere.
14. **Scripture-first** without proof-text mechanics. Four relations are kept distinct: Scripture teaches; RS is consistent with; RS proposes a mechanism compatible with; Scripture provokes a question RS explores.

## 5. Explicitly not claimed

- That the substrate is spatial, or that it is not.
- That T0 is a literal realm, store or place.
- That T-C-R is packet transport or physical travel.
- That stochasticity is an agent, or that it proves free will.
- That a successful physical model uniquely fixes metaphysics, or that mathematical description is ontology.
- That coherence proves RS.
- That Scripture supplies a substrate geometry.
- That evil was necessary. Chapter 14's addition argues the reverse from Genesis 2–3. Any counterfactual about how the knowledge of good and evil would otherwise have been gained is labelled speculation.
- That resurrection is natural substrate reconstruction.

## 6. Test of the causal formulation against the stochastic work

The working formulation was:

> A causal relation exists where a distinction in one relational condition modifies the admissible actualisation or consequential state of another through a distinction-preserving dependency.

Checked against the constraint laboratory's later generations (branch `research/constraint-lab-v0.8c-mechanism` and `-v0.8d-two-gate-composition`):

- A current triadic gate changes the weighting of the next pairwise event, an exact non-zero current-context dependence. That fits "modifies the admissible actualisation", in its weighting form.
- The initial value of the same gate is forgotten. Information about it in the pair state falls below `1e-10` within 64 pair epochs. On the formulation, the initial distinction stops being causal for the pair state once no consequence can depend on it. That matches the T-C-R test: a distinction that can make no downstream difference has not been carried.
- Two such gates compose by adding log-weights, with consequences coupled through normalisation. This is a case where two distinctions modify one actualisation without either being "produced".
- Prohibitions (count-gated rules in Generation 2) change which states are reachable at all. Soft weights do not open new supports. That fits the distinction between exclusion and weighting.

**Conclusion:** the formulation survives as a working hypothesis. It is adopted in Chapter 7 with that status. It does not establish that nature works this way. The laboratory grammar is the experimenter's.
