## 1. Reader read and budget

- **Reader.** The book's own stated reader (Preface): "an intelligent reader with no specialist training in physics, philosophy or theology who is willing to think carefully". In narrative-arc terms, a *Newcomer* with a serious interest. They know Scripture better than physics, and know neither the project's vocabulary nor its research history.
- **One next step.** To understand the proposal well enough to judge it: to see what is claimed, at what status, and what would count against it.
- **Budget.** Main path (front matter and Chapters 1–15): 40,219 words at v2.4 by the metrics script, about 201 minutes at 200 wpm. Appendices A–E are on demand (7,368 words). Target: the main path is no longer than at v2.4 *after* the GT and laboratory material is added. The new material is paid for by cutting repetition, recaps and research history in the Preface and Chapters 1–8.
- **Cognitive load.** v2.4 was highest in Chapter 2 (eleven claim kinds, six coherence tests, six layers of knowing before the central claim) and Chapters 7–8 (the grammar stated twice, and a four-problem map recapped twice). Target: M throughout. A chapter adds at most two new terms, and the GT material adds two: *succession* and *gathering*.
