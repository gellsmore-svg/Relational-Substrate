## Adversarial counterexamples applied to the new material

1. **Twelve opportunities, four actualisations** (audit countermodel 1). Used directly as the ferry example in Ch6. It separates opportunity cadence from actualisation cadence. The example's chooser was flagged (physical order does not choose) and a clause added.
2. **Possible but forbidden** (countermodel 2). Genesis 3:6 in Ch13 distinguishes physical and operational admissibility from moral admissibility.
3. **2 + 2 = 5** (countermodel 3). Ch10 locates the error in one relation and keeps the guard that an intelligible surrounding does not make a false relation true.
4. **Time-symmetric physics.** A physicist's objection to succession as a premise: most fundamental equations run equally well backwards. Answered in Ch6: equations describe admissible histories; succession concerns which occurred (description vs ontology).
5. **Infinite sequences.** An objection to the grounding argument: infinite mathematical sequences are consistent. Ch3 states the argument as one about a claimed ultimate ground, not about mathematics.
6. **Fall mechanism fork.** The author's thesis (primitive realignment) is stated as the author's; parameter-change and new-state readings are kept as research, not substituted.
7. **Animal death before humans.** The fossil record's conventional chronology conflicts with post-Fall animal mortality. Named as an open problem in Ch14, Ch15 and App E.10, by the author's decision; not resolved and not hidden.
8. **Every suffering as punishment.** A misreading invited by "creation teaches decay and loss". Guarded with John 9:3.
9. **Premise immunity.** Making B's *that* a premise could shield RS from evidence. Ch9's counter-evidence keeps B's mechanism and its comparative argument exposed, and says the premise rests on other grounds.
