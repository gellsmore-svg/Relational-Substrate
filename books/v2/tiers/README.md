# Tiered editions of *Coherent Biblical Ontology*

Three shorter editions derived from the second edition, revision 2.7. The source of truth is `books/v2/manuscript/` together with its governance record, Appendix E (`21-appendix-e-ontology.md`). Revision 2.7 itself serves as the bachelor's tier. Nothing in a tier may claim more, or claim it more firmly, than the source does. If a tier and the source disagree, the source wins and the tier is regenerated.

These replace the May 2026 tier files in `~/RS-claude/` (Beginner v1, GCSE v2, A-level v2, Bachelor's v2). Those were built on the first-edition framework: the ontological stack, the transformation tier, matter as settled "knots" and the vorton taught as fact. None of that framework is carried over.

Method: the `narrative-arc` skill (one reader role per tier, budget set before drafting, integrity set kept through scoping), `before-the-message` (per-phrase recipient check; scoring loop on openings, closes and heavy passages) and `human-book-readability` (review in [`reviews/`](reviews/)).

Budgets allow ±10%. Read-time is at 200 words a minute.

## What each tier leaves out, and why

**Beginner.** Omits the formal labels (T0, T1, T-C-R as an abbreviation, A/B/C), the six layers of knowing, local/regional/global coherence as named scopes, comparative probability, invariants, causal states, provenance as a term, identity organiser and coherer, the grammar of emergence, coordination, the Gravity/Time models, the serious alternative ontologies, true and inferential logic, and the cadence vocabulary. Each is either a refinement the beginner's next step does not need or a technical bridge whose *consequence* is kept in plain words: for example, "same kind is not the same one" carries the equivalence/individual distinction, and "the stone carries its history" carries provenance and memory.

**Intermediate.** Keeps the arc and most named distinctions at one level of depth. Omits T0/T1 labels, explicit and implicit constraints as terms, Barandes's indivisible processes, causal states, the laser and synergetics, the quantum field theory distinctions, the serious alternative ontologies by name, the formal definitions of true and inferential logic, proof and coherence factor, the grounding regress, and the Gravity/Time models beyond one sentence.

**A-level.** Keeps every named distinction the argument needs. Omits research-history detail (dates of model generations, the metal-fatigue study's specifics beyond its lesson), most literary illustration, and Appendices A to D, to which it points.

## Build

- Chapters: `books/v2/tiers/<tier>/NN-*.md`.
- Assemble: `python3 books/v2/tools/assemble_tiers.py` writes `books/coherent-biblical-ontology-<tier>.md`; `--check` fails if any is stale.
- Covers: `books/v2/tools/build_tier_covers.py` writes `books/v2/tiers/covers/<tier>-front.{svg,png}`.
- EPUBs: `npm run books:epub:tiers` builds and checks `books/epub/coherent-biblical-ontology-<tier>.epub`.
- Review: [`reviews/tiers-review.md`](reviews/tiers-review.md).
