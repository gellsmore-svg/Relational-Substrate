# Chapter 7: Transmit, Carry, Receive

Sunlight falls on the stone; its grey body and pale vein return light differently; some reaches your eye; molecules, cells and nerves change; you see the stone. Nothing moved from stone to head, yet a distinction in the stone became a distinction in you. The book names what that requires **Transmit, Carry, Receive** (T-C-R), and proposes it as the grammar of consequential manifestation: the logical structure by which any distinction in created order becomes consequential to another state.

## The minimal grammar

```text
distinction → preserved dependency → consequence
```

- **Transmit**: a distinction in one relational condition is capable of constraining something other than itself.
- **Carry**: the dependency between distinction and consequence preserves enough of the distinction for the consequence to depend on it. The carrier need not be an object; at the most primitive level the relation itself may carry.
- **Receive**: another state becomes actual, or its range of actualisation is constrained, because of the source distinction. Reception is consequence, not arrival.

**Test:** if two different source states could never make any difference to any consequence, the distinction has not been carried.

The minimal grammar presupposes little: no travel, no measurable space to travel through, no intermediate object, no encoding and decoding, no layers. The source need not be depleted (telling your name; a lighthouse), and reception need not occur exactly once. Each of these occurs in particular cases; none belongs to the grammar.

## Transmission and carriage

What reaches you is not "light" in general but the **source distinction** the stone makes in it; were the stone identical to its surroundings in every way light registers, it would be invisible. To transmit is to be capable of constraining something beyond oneself, by emission, copying, exposure, modulation or simply standing in a relation on which something depends. Transmission is governed by admissibility: an atom emits only at energies its structure allows, hence each element's spectral fingerprint.

Carriage need not be travel. A pebble's ripples reach the bank though no water crosses the pond; signal fires relay news though no fire travels, each lit *in dependence on* the last; a telephone call survives conversion through current, radio, glass and current again. What persists is **the relevant distinction, preserved by a chain of dependency**. The *carrier* is whatever bears that dependency at a stage, and carriers are substitutable: a carrier can preserve consequence without preserving its implementation. At substrate depth, where two conditions are constitutively related, the relation is the dependency and nothing further crosses.

Chapter 6 does work here. A carrier must preserve the source distinction's **relevant equivalence class**: whatever the receiver's possible responses can tell apart. A telephone line can drop vocal texture but not the difference between "yes" and "no". **Provenance** must be carried where the receiver's response depends on it, as with signatures or chains of custody. Carriage is constrained, degrading under noise, and is persistence of something, against some disturbance, over some time.

## Reception and compatibility

Light passing through a car window leaves the window unchanged in any way dependent on the stone; the light arrived but the distinction was not received. At the retina, a molecule changes shape on absorption, starting a cascade: reception. A letter unopened on the mat has arrived; read and acted on, it has been received.

**Receiver compatibility** has two sides. Every receiver has a range of registrable distinctions (the eye admits visible light, not radio), so reception too is governed by admissibility. And receivers differ in their response to repetition: a thermostat receives continuously, an ear integrates, a switch already on is idempotent, a choir is changed more by each rehearsal, and a bank ledger requires exactly-once reception. Exactly-once is a property of transactional receivers, not a law of reception. An FM broadcast shows how much compatibility involves: the programme is carried in the *modulation* of a carrier frequency, and recovery requires both tuning and a matched demodulator. Five things are distinct: carrier frequency, modulation, preserved organisation, the receiver's compatible arrangement, and the programme reconstructed. Hence: **consequential reception depends on compatibility between the organisation in which a distinction is preserved and the organisation of the receiver.** **Status:** ontological inference.

## Identity organiser and identity coherer

When what passes is an organised identity, two functions appear. An **identity organiser** is a relational organisation or process that establishes, structures or constrains distinctions so that they can participate in a coherent, consequential identity (a modulator; a cello's strings and body). An **identity coherer** is a relational organisation capable of preserving or reconstructing consequentially relevant identity from variations received through a compatible interface (a demodulator; an ear and brain). Three cautions: neither is a substance, field or vibration by definition; cohering is not confined to receivers (frequency modulation itself confers robustness); and one process can do both. Neither belongs to the minimal grammar. The working reading is that they are T-C-R's transmitting and receiving roles specialised to identities. **Status:** RS working hypothesis; developing research concepts.

A distinction may be transmitted and carried without consequential reception: starlight falling on nothing able to register it. The propagation is real; the chain is incomplete.

## Why the grammar recurs

T-C-R nests (the nerve impulse is its own chain), composes (one retinal image from many rays) and is coordinated (depth from two eyes' related images). The three-part form recurs in light, DNA replication, nerves, speech, law and liturgy. Coincidence is one explanation. The book proposes another: if A is genuinely consequential for B, something about A must be able to make a difference to B, that difference must be preserved, and B must differ because of it, which already is T-C-R. **T-C-R is the operational form of consequential relation itself.** It is also **recursive**: every receiver is a source for a further relation, so consequential relation is a network in which every present state is the reception of earlier distinctions and a source of later ones. That is how actuality carries history and shapes reachability.

**Status:** RS working hypothesis (stronger form). Supported by recurrence and economy; weakened by a genuine consequence not describable as a preserved distinction, or by showing the recurrence is better explained as convergent design. A shared grammar is a structural analogy, not an identity of nature.

## Cause as consequential relation

In a deterministic picture a cause produces its effect. In a stochastic world a distinction can still be causal by excluding next states, enabling others, changing likelihoods, constraining correlations, preserving an invariant or altering later accessibility. **Working formulation:** *a causal relation exists where a distinction in one relational condition modifies the admissible actualisation, or the consequential state, of another through a distinction-preserving dependency.* It was checked against the simplest models, where a three-way condition weights the next two-way event without determining it, while that condition's initial value soon ceases to matter because no dependency preserves it. **Status:** working hypothesis.

The account also disciplines "enabled", which can mean **precedence** (came first), **competitive clearance** (removed competitors; the project's model reports call this "opportunity", a word the book reserves for Chapter 4's sense), **rate** (changed the other's tendency) or **support** (made it possible at all). Only rate and support change admissibility or tendency, and only support is the work of a forbidding rule. In the later models most soft "enabling" proved to be competitive clearance.

## Manifestation, and its status

For the substrate to be manifest as material and experienced reality, there must be a **consequence-preserving relationship** between distinctions in the underlying order and the world we meet. That minimal claim is all the book makes. It does not assume a hidden object transformed into a visible one through layers; the two might be descriptions of one fact, resolutions of one order, or related by a mapping not yet understood.

**Status overall.** Emission, propagation and absorption are established physics, which T-C-R organises without replacing; it has not been shown to predict anything the physics does not. Open: whether created order generates its own carriers from minimal relations (in every model so far the carriers were designed), and the architecture of manifestation.

## T-C-R across categories

> `So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it.`
> *Isaiah 55:11*

Isaiah's image is physical rain; its subject is God's word. Paul traces sending, preaching, hearing and believing (Romans 10:14-17). The form is that of something made available, carried and received. On Chapter 1's four relations, this is at least a **structural analogy**; whether created consequence was also designed to **represent** how God's word works is a question about creation's purpose (Chapter 16). It is not identity: God's word is not a physical signal, and faith is not the absorption of light.

## What this chapter establishes

- T-C-R's minimal grammar presupposes no travel, encoding or layers; reception is consequence.
- Carriage preserves the relevant equivalence class; reception depends on receiver compatibility.
- Identity organiser and coherer are functional roles (RS hypothesis); T-C-R as the form of consequential relation and an account of cause are working hypotheses.
- Cross-category use is structural analogy, possibly representation, never identity.
