# Relational Substrate / Nexology — Primitive-Level Recursive Coherence Audit
**Scope:** Detailed concept-by-concept extraction from the conversation of 31 October 2026, including corrections made during it. **Not a score of a prior summary.**
**Unit count:** 186 separately scored conceptual units across 14 coherence clusters. **Method:** atomic extraction → vector scoring → dependency challenge → cross-cluster repair → maturity assessment.

**Recursive scoring:** the stored data includes an initial vector and a challenged/revised vector for each unit. The report displays the revised vector. Changes follow a calibration pass that penalises definitional vagueness, domain-bridging leaps, unsupported discrimination, and hidden dependencies. The dependency passes then audit the result and name remaining forks.

## Reading the vectors
Each unit has a six-part ordinal vector **[C,P,A,R,D,U]**, each 0–5. **C:** internal compatibility/coherence with the developing framework; **P:** definitional precision; **A:** anchoring in its *declared* domain (deduction, observation, Scripture, or explicitly adopted metaphysical premise); **R:** explanatory reach across relationships; **D:** discriminating/constraint-producing power; **U:** unresolved dependency or vulnerability (higher is worse). These are **research triage judgments**, not percentages of truth or empirical probabilities. Biblical anchoring does not automatically establish a physical mechanism; an ontological proposal is not downgraded merely for not being empirically observable.
For kinds: **Premise** means an explicit starting commitment; **Definition** is proposed usage; **Thesis** is argued relationship; **Hypothesis** is a specific possible mechanism; **Interpretation** relates theological/textual ideas; **Textual anchor** is directly supported biblical content; **Analogy** illustrates but does not entail; **Open question** names an unresolved discriminator; **Example** tests a distinction. A high U on an open question means important work, not a failed claim.

## Pass V — book-ready definitions (provisional, preserve open nodes)
- **True logic.** Ultimate global coherence, perfectly comprehended and applicable by its personal source; coherence and perfect knowledge are analytically distinguishable yet inseparable in the divine nature on the adopted theological premise.
- **Inferential logic.** The recursive refinement of partial understanding towards true logic through the discovery and application of relational coherence within the boundaries of created runtime order.
- **Logic.** The necessary relational structure governing consistency, implication, exclusion and the admissibility of possibilities.
- **Reasoning.** The application or traversal of represented relational structures to derive, evaluate or revise conclusions.
- **Logical reasoning.** The attempted or valid application of logical relationships to infer possibilities and consequences within a constrained order.
- **Explicitly intrinsic constraint.** A foundational constraint belonging to the primitive configuration of created runtime order.
- **Implicitly intrinsic constraint.** A conditionally necessary constraint entailed by a particular derived relational configuration.
- **Opportunity.** A distinguishable occasion at which conditions allow one or more candidate actualizations; how an unactualized occasion is individuated remains open.
- **Stochastic actualization.** The realization of an admissible outcome where the determining constraints do not uniquely specify which eligible outcome will occur; whether this is ontic rather than epistemic remains a research assumption.
- **Opportunity cadence.** The ordered pattern of distinguishable opportunities for actualization, including irregular patterns.
- **Actualization cadence.** The ordered pattern of actual occurrences, distinguishable from their opportunity-generating occasions.
- **Time [candidate].** A relative quantification of ordered opportunity cadence against a reference cadence; not final until premetric order and recurrence have independent definitions.
- **Recursive inference.** The iterative derivation and evaluation of conclusions that may revise the premises, constraints and possibilities governing subsequent inference.
- **Inferential precision [candidate].** The quality with which a finite inference model discriminates candidate relations, traces warrant and constraints, and survives challenge while retaining correctable uncertainty.
- **Coherence factor [candidate].** An inferred or observed relational condition admitted for reuse in subsequent reasoning with explicit scope, provenance, dependencies and revision status.
- **Relational error.** An incorrect assignment or application of relationship within an intelligible structure whose other elements or operations may preserve genuine coherence.
- **Wonder.** A conscious creaturely response to encountered coherence whose intelligibility invites further discovery without implying exhaustive comprehension.
