## 2. Essential authorial positions to preserve

The following statements are **paraphrased anchors and selected direct wording** drawn from this conversation. Consult the original before quoting in the manuscript.

- **Scope and naming:** Use **created runtime order**, not **universe**, when describing the author's ontology. Created runtime order is not identical to everything that exists; some realities lie beyond finite observational capacity. Never anthropomorphise created reality as conscious or as spontaneously 'becoming' capable of consciousness.
- **True logic:** Ultimate global coherence: complete comprehension of all coherence factors and the capacity to apply every logical relation, inseparable from the personal divine source and God's nature. Distinguish these analytically only insofar as that helps explain their inseparability in God. Creaturely minds do not possess true logic exhaustively.
- **Author's preferred definition of inferential logic (quote):** “Inferential logic is the recursive refinement of partial understanding towards true logic through the discovery and application of relational coherence within the boundaries of created runtime order.”
- **Research objective:** Increase **inferential precision**, not claim possession of absolute proof. What is conventionally called 'proof' is understood as acceptance of a sufficiently warranted inferred relation into the developing framework as a reusable **candidate coherence factor**, open to recursive revision. Retain legitimate conditional deduction without making 'established' or 'proven' a reflexive gate for ontology.
- **Inference and methods:** Experiments, observations, reasoning and philosophical inquiry are contributions to **inference**, not peers arranged alongside inference. Recursive inference must be able to revise its starting premises and its interpretations of foundational constraints.
- **Logic / reasoning:** Distinguish the necessary relational **structure** (logic) from its **application or traversal** (reasoning). 'Logical reasoning' is the consequential application of logic. At higher orders call it **relational inference**. Do not conflate logical necessity with deterministic selection of a unique actualization.
- **Primitive / derived constraints:** **Explicitly intrinsic** constraints belong to the primitive created order; **implicitly intrinsic** constraints arise as necessary consequences of derived configurations. They are distinct as organisational categories and expressions of underlying coherence. A stochastic actualization can establish a configuration whose resulting constraints are necessary. Maintain distinction between physical impossibility and morally prohibited yet physically possible choice.
- **Fixed primitive and grounding:** The created primitive is foundational/fixed relative to creaturely runtime and deliberately specified by its Creator; it is not immutable to God. The author rejects an endlessly deferred hierarchy of purely derivative explanatory grounds as failing foundational coherence. Preserve his stronger objection and its dependency argument; do not substitute the analyst's preference for general philosophical permissiveness. Equally, do not present a software analogy as if it independently proved a universal logical theorem.
- **Opportunity / actualization / cadence:** Distinguish **possibility**, **opportunity**, **admissibility**, **actualization**, **opportunity cadence**, and **actualization cadence**. Opportunities may recur without the corresponding actualization; these cadences need not coincide. Time is tentatively a **relative quantification of cadence**, requiring a noncircular account of ordering/reference and opportunity individuation. Time direction and irreversibility through persistent relational consequences/TCR remain research questions, not settled definitions.
- **TCR:** Transmit–Carry–Receive describes consequential relational propagation; it may be a recurring structure across primitive and conscious inference, and possibly across spiritual significance and created representation. A correspondence is not automatically a mechanism. Preserve the distinction and research it.
- **Two domains and personhood:** An intrinsically coherent, non-conscious created runtime order represents aspects of spiritual relational structure; a conscious domain can apprehend and respond to coherence, including in relationship with God and in moral agency. They converge in **creaturely personhood**. Do not reduce consciousness to complexity, call created reality conscious, or say the physical order gains a capacity not included in design.
- **Fall and divine judgment:** The author's proposed interpretation is that God's curse/judgment in Genesis 3 deliberately **realigns the primitives** of the created runtime order in relation to the consequences of spiritual disobedience, so that creation remains a truthful instructor. A possible distinction between changed primitives and a state transition under original primitives can be investigated, but do not quietly substitute the latter as the author's thesis. Treat mechanism as an open inferential programme rather than inserting generic 'not proven' disclaimers.
- **Creation as revelation, beauty and wonder:** Created relational structures instruct creatures in spiritual relationships. Beauty is fundamentally spiritual and may be represented through the created runtime order and apprehended consciously; ongoing inference and wonder relate to finite participation in truth. Spiritual order is intrinsically of divine coherence; created physical order reflects it.
- **Biblical connection:** John 14:6 ('the way, the truth, and the life'), John 10:30, Genesis 3, Romans 8, Romans 1:20 and relevant passages are part of an inferential theological synthesis. Separate **the wording of Scripture**, **the author's theological interpretation**, and **the proposed substrate mechanism** without excluding any category. Do not imply Genesis itself explicitly details microphysical primitive changes.
- **Error / distortion:** Intelligible falsehood is dependent upon real relational coherence. In **2+2=5**, the quantities, addition operation and question are coherent; the incorrect equality is a local relational error. Preserve coherent substructures from failed hypotheses. Trial and error can increase inferential precision by identifying exclusions or correcting even the question.
- **Faith:** Faith can be understood as a committed choice along the way towards truth without advance knowledge of outcomes, with trust in a coherent created order grounded in God. Do not conflate every inference with explicit religious faith.

Treat score precision, transitive dependency counts and cluster medians as navigational aids rather than authority.
