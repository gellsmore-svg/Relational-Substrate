# 9. Critical epistemic refinement from Review 005

A nonzero additive residual:

```text
I = Q_TP - Q_T - Q_P + Q_0
```

does **not by itself** establish a uniquely higher-order interaction.

A committor is a nonlinear function of the transition kernel.

Two purely pairwise constraints can also generate:

```text
I != 0
```

through ordinary constraint composition, transition normalisation, and multi-step path structure.

The fact that all 2,304 targeted systems have nonzero `I` makes this control especially important.

Therefore the correct v0.5 conclusion is:

> **Constraint composition is strongly non-additive in the lower-order reconfiguration observable, and a higher-order-conditioned component can make that non-additivity depend strongly on hidden higher-order relational state.**

Do **not yet** conclude:

> higher-order conditioning creates a uniquely new kind of non-additivity.

v0.6 must test that distinction.

# Governing conceptual question

v0.5 established that two-rule constraint composition can generate a large non-additive change in a lower-order reconfiguration committor, and that the hidden triadic configuration can become more influential in the combined system.

v0.6 must now determine **why**.

The strongest question is:

> **When action geometry and weights are held fixed, does a genuine higher-order relational gate produce behaviour that is distinguishable from deleting the gate entirely or replacing it with matched lower-order pair predicates?**

And independently:

> **Does that distinction survive when hard prohibition is replaced by finite stochastic weights?**

Those two questions come before any increase in ontology, dimensionality, history, cardinality, or event semantics.

The goal is not to accumulate complexity.

The goal is to isolate the smallest relational distinction that actually carries explanatory work.
