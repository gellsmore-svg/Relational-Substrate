> **When fine labels and fine pair configuration are hidden, does the history disappear—or does it survive in the grammar of the coarse sequence?**

The strongest clean test is not:

```text
H versus neutral.
```

It is:

```text
true H process
versus
its matched stationary current-state Kpi process.
```

Those models agree exactly on:

```text
one-time material structure
+
adjacent one-step material statistics.
```

So if they diverge at:

```text
three-time or longer graph-class / edge-count words,
```

the lost information is genuinely process-level.

OpenThetical Research then asks what kind of process difference survived:

```text
density path
shape sequence
both
neither
or something unexpected.
```

The purpose of 8J is to let that answer determine the next direction without changing the ontology first.
