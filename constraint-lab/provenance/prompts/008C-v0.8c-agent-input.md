# 92. Permanent principle

Add to the programme methodology:

> **A finding has multiple dimensions. Magnitude, robustness, persistence, explanatory reach, and structural role must not be collapsed into a single strength score. A small or fragile phenomenon may be structurally essential; a large robust phenomenon may be mechanistically generic. Preserve literal observations and let later relational evidence establish significance.**

This principle should govern all future coherence-map updates.
