The ordered history pair:

```text
(h1,h2)
```

contains relational structure in its own right.

A candidate event e can stand to that ordered pair as:

```text

adjacent
disjoint
```

```text

```

Thus depth-two history introduces a local relational grammar that cannot be reduced merely to:

```text
"which old edge should be repeated?"
```

```text

or leaves distinctions beyond every matched first-order H1 representation.
```

```text
no
mixed by role
or unexpectedly equivalent.
```

All four outcomes are scientifically useful.
