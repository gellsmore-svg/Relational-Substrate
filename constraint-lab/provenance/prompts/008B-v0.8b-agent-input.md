# 31. Exact-law CMI under three weightings

Calculate:

```text
I(T ; E | P)
```

where:

```text
T = current 4-bit triad
E = next pair slot 0..5
P = full labelled 6-bit pair mask
```

under three explicitly distinct state weightings.

## A. Balanced-law weighting

Weight every:

```text
pair mask × triad state
```

equally before applying the exact pair-slot law.

This measures intrinsic triad dependence of the rule independent of occupancy.

## B. A0 empirical event-source weighting

Use the empirical distribution of pre-pair-event full states from the A0 event streams, but replace the sampled one-hot next slot with the **exact pair-slot probability vector** at that state.

This removes next-event sampling noise while retaining the finite-horizon states actually visited.

## C. Exact stationary pair-event weighting

Compute the exact stationary distribution `pi(s)` of the full-event Markov chain.

Form the event-weighted joint distribution:

```text
Pr(s, E=e | event is pair)
∝
pi(s) * P_full_event(E=e | s)
```

over pair slots.

Calculate CMI from that deterministic joint distribution.

This measures long-run experienced current-context dependence.

# Governing methodological principle

Generation 8A showed that the symmetry basis had to respect the dynamics.

Generation 8B applies the same principle to statistics:

> **The estimator is part of the observation apparatus and must itself be calibrated.**

A non-zero empirical information value does not automatically mean that the dynamics carry that information.

When the model contains exact zero-information controls, use them.

When the state space permits exact transition-law calculation, use it.

The aim is not to rescue or eliminate a higher-order signal.

The aim is to determine what the relational system actually contains after:

```text
sampling noise
estimator bias
projection loss
and true transition-law dependence
```

have been separated as cleanly as possible.
