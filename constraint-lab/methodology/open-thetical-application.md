## Direction and complementary possibilities

OTR means coherence-guided, recursively adaptive directed exploration. The
laboratory declares a meaningful basin and several live possibilities before
choosing a bounded complementary palette. In 8I the basin is the smallest
label-covariant history x present interaction with H_pair=1. No topology,
occupancy, persistent effect, optimisation target or larger ontology is required.

Family A reads candidate/history relation without current action context.
It complements B by exposing temporal/history effects in isolation from action
direction; it does not isolate history x present coupling. Family B reads current
action direction without history. It complements A/C by showing structural
effects possible already at H0; it does not establish historical capability.
Family C conjoins these features. Contrasts with A/B isolate what the conjunction
changes, but do not uniquely identify a causal mechanism or exclude all other
current-state comparators. Uniform-history KU is one declared comparator.

## Freeze boundary

Round selection is Mode B; local exact identities and discriminations may be
Mode A. Every scientific round fixes features, cells, clocks, projections,
estimands, arithmetic, tolerances, work counts, budget and stopping rules in a
committed and pushed plan before target calculations. Neutral, historical and
synthetic plumbing exposure is declared. Adaptation changes the next plan, never
an already observed plan. Scientific nulls are retained; engineering failures
are diagnosed before interpreting cells.

## Observation retention and coherence

Publish the complete palette and retain null, weak, fragile, equivalent,
countervailing and unexpected observations. Keep magnitude, robustness, temporal
character, projection and epistemic layer separate; no summed score or controller
leaderboard. The possibility ledger retains its original entries; observations
later record which possibilities they constrain, split or leave unresolved.

The canonical findings/relations ledger remains the permanent record. Mathematical
proof, finite exact result, numerical result, model inference and RS/global
interpretation are distinct. An OTR adoption node is methodology, not evidence
for RS. Shared use of OTR does not merge the laboratory with GT.

## Recursive selection

After a complete bounded atlas, review all outcomes and generate at least five
next probes. Choose at most two complementary probes for illumination rather
than effect size. Any executed I1 is independently frozen. An honest stop is
permitted when no additional probe within the round is justified; preserve the
remaining questions and explicit reason. The user authorises stopping after I0.
Neither deeper H nor L nor scale opens automatically.
