The JSONL catalogue is canonical; its Markdown and dependency diagram are generated by
`python -m rs_constraint_lab.formal --generate`. Run `--verify` to validate references, proof obligations, dependency acyclicity and generated views.

Each statement has one epistemic status, not an overall score. Catalogue entries link to historical coherence findings without changing their original statements or epistemic layers. Algebraic results concern the declared finite model, not physical reality.

Theorem IDs are namespaced `CL-Txx`: the RS book already uses T1-T4 for ontology axioms. The separate coherence ledger records research history; the catalogue records statements and proof dependencies.

Generation 8H adds the first executed H example. [History as state](history.md)
separates ordinary material x from finite register h, proves the same-state
non-compilability boundary, and restores Markov form on augmented state.
Its verified appendix extends the catalogue without rewriting 8G statements.

Generation 8I adds [historical role grammar](history-role-grammar.md), with
generic pair closure, S4 quotients, history-only uniformity, order/projection
identities and the separately frozen I1 mechanism results. Its research selection
uses [the OTR laboratory adapter](../methodology/open-thetical-application.md).
Historical formal entries remain intact; cell-specific numbers stay in tables.

Generation 8J adds [coarse process memory](coarse-process-memory.md): stationary
H/Kpi matching, passive-register paths, invariant-window label silence, the
class-word information chain and separately frozen class-pair/edge-role
mechanisms. Exact process words reuse the 8I palette without a new ontology.
