## Ordered local invariant basis

The action sends all three pair coordinates by the same vertex permutation.
An invariant real function is constant on each orbit; conversely an arbitrary
constant on each orbit is invariant. Orbit indicators have disjoint nonempty
supports, are independent and span this restricted invariant-function space.
The all-ADJ descriptor has two fibres: a common vertex (STAR) versus a cycle
on three vertices (TRIANGLE). Candidate presence is separately invariant;
both presence values are realisable for every triple. Thus the restricted
direction extension has twice the orbit-indicator dimension. This is NOT a
basis of arbitrary functions of the whole graph, triads, time or deeper past.

## Pair-event closure

At fixed (P,h1,h2), let six pair weights be w_e and W=sum w_e. The four triad
events have unit weight and preserve P,h1,h2 and therefore all pair weights.
Summing triad-only excursions yields sum_(n>=0) (4/(W+4))^n*w_e/(W+4)=w_e/W.
No raw triad mask enters. Sentinels give all-unit rows; recurrent dynamics
update (P,i,j) to (P xor 2^e,e,i). No full 36864-state dense kernel is needed.

## Positive support and period

Every event word is positive. To reach target (Q,i,j) from (P,a,b), first
choose any XOR-toggle prefix ending at Q xor 2^j xor 2^i, then append j,i.
The resulting endpoint is (Q,i,j). Hence irreducibility is controller-
independent for positive weights. Parity flips each event. At diagonal
history (i,i), toggling i twice returns in two steps. Irreducibility gives
common period exactly two. Stationarity is not pointwise parity convergence.

## History-only negative control

Let q(e|i,j) not read P and let nu be stationary for history update
(i,j)->(e,i). Destination (P',e,i) has incoming mass
sum_j nu(i,j)q(e|i,j)/64=nu(e,i)/64. Therefore Uniform(P) times nu is
stationary. Positive support makes it unique. This proves material silence
and material/history independence, not temporal silence. It applies to
ANY roles and is stronger than SECOND-RECENT's special uniform joint.

## Matched H1 comparisons

KU averages normalised rows uniformly in older history, not raw weights.
Kpi uses actual conditional pi(h2|P,h1). Let s=(P,h1). Then
pi_s(s)Kpi(s,s')=sum_(z projecting to s) pi(z)T(z,s'), which proves exact
adjacent joint equality and stationarity. Deterministic material projections

strong lumpability or all-path compilation. Kpi always captures occupancy,
so occupancy alone cannot establish divergence from every H1 description.
