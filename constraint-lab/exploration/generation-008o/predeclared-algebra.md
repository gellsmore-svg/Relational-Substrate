# Symbolic Derivations Before F0, Not Factor-Census Results

With Ares=(I-Q)^-1 and Mres=(I-QPi)^-1, multiply the proposed difference on
the left by I-Q. The identity QPi Mres=Mres-I gives

```text
(I-Q)(Ares-Pi Mres) = I-Pi Mres+QPi Mres = (I-Pi)Mres.
Ares-Pi Mres = Ares(I-Pi)Mres.
Delta R = a Ares(I-Pi)Mres Rexit = U V = U W.
```

The algebra needs the inverses but not Pi idempotence. The reset interpretation
does need LC=I and Pi=CL. Finite irreducibility and positive conditional lifting
make K=LTC irreducible. Nonzero spectra of QPi=(QC)L and LQC match, so both
killed resolvents exist for a nonempty fibre-compatible return set.

For j=n-2, the general finite-length identity is

```text
Q^j - Pi(QPi)^j = sum_{l=0}^j Q^l (I-Pi) (QPi)^(j-l).
```

For this history update, successor S'=(P',e) reconstructs predecessor material
P' xor 2^e. Conditioning a stationary incoming flow on source class and route
therefore restricts only S'; a(z') is pi2(z') times a coarse indicator divided
by pi(source). Thus a=aPi. This proves a(I-Pi)Rexit=0 before target aggregation.
It does NOT imply U=0. Odd returns vanish by parity. At four events:

```text
Delta F4 = a(I-Pi)(QPi)^2 Rexit
          + aQ(I-Pi)QPi Rexit + aQ^2(I-Pi)Rexit.
```

The first term vanishes by entrance balance; the other two must be evaluated,
not assumed nonzero. Summing destination is postcomposition by the all-ones
column. Its zero need not be a symmetry or universal row-sum conservation at
a fixed length. Only total eventual return mass is forced to one.

The simultaneous S4 quotient preserves invariant rows, columns and products,
including conditional reset, because stationary mass is invariant and fibres
are equivariant. Quotient rows are orbit MASSES. Full ranks of I-Pi and
Ares(I-Pi) are n_Z(B_d)-n_S(B_d); invariant-sector ranks use orbit counts.
The full rank of W equals its sector rank since its columns are invariant.
No full-space rank is inferred from the sector alone.
