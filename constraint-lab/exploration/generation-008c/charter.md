# Generation 008C charter

Generation 8C is Mode B, open relational exploration. It is not a trajectory census and it is not a matched-control experiment. No new stochastic scientific trajectories are run. No parameters are fitted. No controls are added.

The question is a mechanism reading of structures already measured in Generations 8, 8A, and 8B:

```text
How does a higher-order triadic condition alter the local lower-order event law, how does visible graph topology convert that local event bias into edge-count versus shape changes, and why is the influence of the initial triadic context rapidly forgotten?
```

The aim is explanatory compression. A higher-order effect that is locally genuine, and an initial-condition footprint that is small and short-lived, should be read from the same exact transition geometry.

Ontology, unchanged: N=4, O=3, G=S=H=L=0, independent hypergraph, alphabet W4.

Round C0 is the only frozen analysis round. It uses exact constraint semantics, exact pair-slot weights, the exact pair-event epoch kernel, exact graph classification, and exact finite-state enumeration. A later round is not defined until C0 has been read.

Graph classes are descriptors of pairwise relation sets. Organisation, repair, routing success, boundary, and preferred graph are not targets and are not scores.

Pass and fail remain appropriate for software, CI, validation, and execution contracts. They are not the vocabulary of the mechanism reading.

The three epistemic layers stay separate: observation, inference, and RS / global-coherence interpretation.
