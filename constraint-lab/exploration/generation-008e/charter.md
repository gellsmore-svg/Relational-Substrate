# Generation 008E charter

Generation 8E is Mode B, open relational exploration. It does not introduce history or meta-constraints. It does not enlarge N. It runs no stochastic trajectory census, fits no parameter, and adds no control family.

The question is:

```text
What genuinely new local relational structure appears when one ordinary constraint can read two higher-order relations conjunctively?
```

Generation 8D exhausted compositions of two primitive one-condition rules (rule cardinality 2, each rule K=2) and found no gate×gate logit coefficient in that subgrammar. Generation 8E holds rule cardinality at 1 and raises expression complexity to K=3: one pair action conditioned on two distinct triad literals, with one finite weight. The two axes are kept separate. Neither is ranked on one scalar.

## Why K before L

The ordinary `Constraint` grammar already accepts several conjunctive conditions in one rule. Before any change to the ontology, such as meta-constraints (`L > 0`) or history (`H > 0`), the round asks what the existing grammar can already express. K controls simultaneous expression complexity. H controls dependence on prior states. L controls constraints on constraints. None of them is used to obtain what another already supplies.

## Scope

- Ontology, unchanged: N=4, O=3, G=S=H=L=0, independent hypergraph.
- The primary family is every single K=3 rule with a pair action and two distinct triad literals, at the four W4 grades.
- Round E0 is the complete local census of that family, together with small algebraic items: the K2-versus-K3 symbolic comparison, the Boolean (Möbius) and corner bases, and targeted XOR and XNOR constructions from two K3 rules. It is frozen before it is executed.
- E1 is optional. It is opened only if E0 leaves a genuine global question, and it is frozen before execution.

## Discipline

Observations, inferences and the RS / global-coherence interpretation stay in separate layers. Magnitude, robustness, persistence, explanatory reach and structural role stay separate. No system is ranked by a scalar. Pass and fail are reserved for software, CI, validation and execution contracts.
