# Generation 008D charter

Generation 8D is Mode B, open relational exploration. It does not introduce history. It does not enlarge N. It does not run another stochastic trajectory census. No parameter is fitted. No control family is added.

The question is the local grammar of two higher-order gates:

```text
When two higher-order relational gates coexist in the same memoryless relational system, what exact algebra of composition appears?
```

One gate was explained in Generation 8C. This generation asks which two-gate systems collapse to a simpler law, which remain genuinely two-gate at the local transition law, how their weights combine in log-weight space and in probability space, and how that local algebra sits relative to graph topology. Topology is not given a new trajectory census here. The Generation 8C distinction is kept: a local-law equivalence does not imply equal source trajectories.

Ontology, unchanged: N=4, O=3, G=S=H=L=0, independent hypergraph. Each primary rule is a pair action conditioned on exactly one triad literal. The systems under study have two such rules. The weight alphabet is the W4 non-neutral grades, used as evaluation points of formulas in two positive multipliers.

Round D0 is the complete local composition census. It is frozen before it is executed. A later round is not defined until D0 has been read. If D0 already yields a coherent grammar, no further round is opened.

Pass and fail remain appropriate for software, CI, validation, and execution contracts. They are not the vocabulary of the composition reading.

The three epistemic layers stay separate: observation, inference, and RS / global-coherence interpretation. Magnitude, robustness, persistence, explanatory reach, and structural role stay separate. No system is ranked by a scalar.
