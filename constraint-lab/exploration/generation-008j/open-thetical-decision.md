## Selected complementary J1

J1-CLASS-PAIR-ROLE: independently derive whether
(class(p xor 2^h),class(p)) is constant and injective on the 28 role orbits.
If it is not, retain collisions and do not claim reconstruction. If it is,
derive the resulting all-past order-at-most-two theorem and compile exact class
rows from every published role kernel; compare with the J0 triples. This would
explain a whole-atlas regularity, not merely one large JS.

J1-SAME-EDGE-ROLE: test the declared (k,R=p_h) block projection for all 23
existing role kernels. For the six SAME cells plus neutral, compare aggregate
rows with a symbolic count-of-present/absent-slots formula, and compare the
reduced chain's edge words with already-frozen J0 words through L6. Retain
ADJ/DISJ failures as boundary witnesses. No new controller, grade or solve.

The two probes jointly address P9/P11/P14/P15/P17: a class-pair reconstruction
and an even coarser action-role quotient. They cannot establish general infinite
edge memory or a universal hidden-state minimisation theorem.
