## Mechanism Alternatives Retained

1. Delayed history correlations alter conditional path weights even without material/history occupancy correlation (ANY).
2. Current direction enables stationary structure, but is not needed for access consequences (ANY counterexample).
3. Narrow sparse/dense excursions may factor out some role distinctions; route-null factorisation is not yet proved.
4. Exact involutions force selected competing probabilities to one half while allowing different MFPT and return rows.
5. Stationary conditional averaging controls one-step flow, not repeated flow composition through an absorbing boundary.
6. Differences need not be universally large or in a single direction; finite grade results do not establish monotonicity.
