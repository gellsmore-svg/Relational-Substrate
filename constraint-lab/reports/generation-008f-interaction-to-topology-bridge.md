## V. Inferences

Pair topology selects which projection can expose a law difference during
routing. Nonlinear normalization, hidden-state motion and event weighting can
turn a centered local logit interaction into marginal occupancy differences.
Matching a balanced mean does not match those weightings. The stationary
factorisation bounds what unlabelled shape can preserve in a single-action,
independent-bit grammar. These conclusions are scoped to this palette.

A large difference may include unmatched lower-order bias; its magnitude alone
does not diagnose interaction order. The three controls supply different questions,
not one scalar interaction-strength measure.

## W. RS / global-coherence interpretation

The bridge adds a dynamical qualification to local expressivity: a new local
coefficient can become transient shape and persistent labelled/edge occupancy,
while some syntactic placement distinctions are erased by independent-bit
symmetry. This is a model result, not evidence that nature uses these logits,
that higher K is more fundamental, or that the ontology should be expanded.
Incidence needs a dynamical coupling before face membership itself can matter.
