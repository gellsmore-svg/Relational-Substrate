## 4. Proof versus physical relevance

- **What T1–T8 are.** Statements about a declared finite stochastic grammar. They use standard Markov-chain mathematics: support graphs, communicating classes, Kolmogorov's criterion.
- **No physical arrow.** One-way release is not a physical arrow of time. Its entropy production is infinite only in the formal sense of a zero reverse rate. Nothing here models what would sustain such a rule.
- **Basin plus arrow under the instrument is a model result.** It is not attraction in any physical sense. The instrument is not a rule of the grammar.
- **Model capability is not physical mechanism.** The claim is only that, in this grammar, the reason ordered-succession rules did not gather was largely the scaffold.
- **No drift or acceleration was demonstrated.** Neither is operationally defined in this programme yet (bridge map, glossary).

## 5. Interpretation, kept separate [RS]

- **A reference is a participant, not a background.** In a relational account, whatever serves as a reference is made of relations too, and it acts on others through the same rules. The model makes this concrete: the held triangle's internal paths bias every attachment. This sits comfortably with a relational ontology that denies a neutral background. It is an interpretation, not evidence.
- **Absence can persist.** "What is not related" can be stable, either because re-formation is slow or because something must first be undone. Persistence of absence is a candidate ingredient of identity (a stable boundary), but no identity construction has been made.
- **Direction and gathering are separable but compatible.** E1 found them decoupled with the triangle scaffold. E3B shows they coexist when the scaffold's own witnesses are removed. The RS reading of GT-F43/F44 (succession and gathering are distinct notions) stands; "they cannot coexist" was never established.
