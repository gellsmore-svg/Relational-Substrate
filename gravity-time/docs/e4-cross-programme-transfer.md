# Candidate transfer note for the Constraint Laboratory (GT-P007)

*These are suggestions only. No laboratory record or branch was edited. The GT results are in the O=2 pair grammar and are not established for O=3.*

## T-5. Witness floors of held structures (for CL-Q04, methodological)

- **Claim (theorem).** A held structure H contributes a fixed, state-independent number of path witnesses to each relation joining it to an outside vertex: Σ over held neighbours c of the endpoint of (deg_H(c) − 1). Only a held single edge is floor-free everywhere.
- **Provenance.** `gravity-time/docs/e4-formal-witness-floors.md` F1, F2. Census-verified in all 48 E4A/E4B cells; plans `f5b3b50` and `9a1c539`.
- **Relevance.** Any laboratory contrast that holds or conditions on a structure should check whether its internal relations satisfy the witness conditions of the rules under test. The O=3 analogue (triad-reading witnesses) is untested.

## T-6. Floor-guided sorting of new relations (for CL-Q04/CL-Q05)

- **Claim (observation).** Under path rules that suppress formation along supported paths (or favour dissolution of supported relations), new relations to a held structure concentrate on its lowest-floor sites.
  - P3: middle against ends, about 2×. Star: centre against leaves, about 4×.
  - A free internal chord can switch a site off.
- **Provenance.** `gravity-time/reports/gt-e4a-atlas.md`, `gt-e4b-atlas.md`; findings GT-F60 to F62.
- **To reproduce.** Compare per-slot occupancy across the orbits of a held structure within single cells, against a NULL with identical geometry.
