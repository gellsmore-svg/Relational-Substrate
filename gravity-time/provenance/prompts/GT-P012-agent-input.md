### 2.2 Derive the exact directional-flux lift

For each occupied macro-cell `z=(ℓ,r)` set

`p_z = Σ_{x:z(x)=z} π(x)`.

For each directed neighboring macro-cell `z→z'` (one local or remote slot toggle), define the **ordered stationary flux**

`J_{z→z'} = Σ_{x:z(x)=z} π(x) Σ_{e:z(x⊕e)=z'} W(x,e)`.

Because full rates are strictly positive, admissible interior fluxes are positive, though tiny bins may be numerically delicate. Define a **macro generator** `Q_Z(z,z')=J_{z→z'}/p_z`. Its stationary law is `p_z` by exact coarse-graining of stationary flow. This does **not** claim the projected full process is dynamically Markov. It matches stationary one-step fluxes only.

Construct an exchangeable-within-pool **lifted full-graph CT generator** `W_NL` as follows. Let `L=|LOCAL|`, `R=|REMOTE|`. At a graph in cell `(ℓ,r)`:

- an absent LOCAL slot has formation rate `J_{(ℓ,r)→(ℓ+1,r)} / [p_(ℓ,r) (L−ℓ)]`;
- a present LOCAL slot has dissolution rate `J_{(ℓ,r)→(ℓ−1,r)} / [p_(ℓ,r) ℓ]`;
- an absent REMOTE slot has formation rate `J_{(ℓ,r)→(ℓ,r+1)} / [p_(ℓ,r) (R−r)]`;
- a present REMOTE slot has dissolution rate `J_{(ℓ,r)→(ℓ,r−1)} / [p_(ℓ,r) r]`;
- boundary moves with zero eligible slots are omitted, not assigned an arbitrary rate.

These are **directed** quantities. Do not average `J_{z→z'}` with `J_{z'→z}` or impose detailed balance without evidence. A loop in the `(ℓ,r)` grid can carry a current. Verify and report whether any current survives projection. **A reversible lift cannot generally preserve both directions when projected currents exist**; quietly symmetrising would falsify the intended control.

Candidate stationary law for the lift:

`π_NL(x)=p_(ℓ(x),r(x)) / [C(L,ℓ(x)) C(R,r(x))]`.

1. `W_NL` is a legal positive single-toggle generator, exchangeable within each slot pool and anchor-equivariant.

3. `NL` matches `p_(ℓ,r)` and *every ordered* stationary macroedge flux `J_{z→z'}`; therefore it also matches the total `k` profile, adjacent `k` traffic, mean CT activity, the event-weighted macro profile and total local/remote stationary event traffic.
