## 1. Mission and governing research question

Carry forward the GT research programme after the completed E1 round (GT-P004). The immediate objective is **not** to produce gravity, prove a preferred theorem, repair a failed basin, or optimise a macroscopic attraction score.

The objective is to investigate, with the smallest informative range of existing relational constraints:

> **What makes relational changes occur in persistent, directionally ordered sequences? Under what circumstances do relationships cooperate, compete, dissolve, enable subsequent changes, or generate qualitatively different modes of succession?**

The striking prior observation is an apparent *handover*: a peripheral probe–probe relation forms, an attachment to a reference structure forms, the peripheral relation dissolves, and the attachment dissolves. The observed orientation was consistent in all 30 tested non-gradient comparisons during E1. But these were **selected families and grades**. They neither prove a general theorem nor establish that the observed order is a causal, transition-enabling mechanism.

Treat the handover as an **entry point into an adjacent space of relational behaviour**, not an effect to preserve or a hypothesis to confirm.

Maintain multiple live possibilities, including incomplete ones:

- The observed orientation might follow from the signs and incidence structure of the PATH rules.
- The orientation might be contingent on grading, support topology, scaffold or the chosen observation frame.
- Local cycle affinity may coexist with weak, absent or reversed *aggregated* handover statistics.
- Dissolution might increase subsequent event probability without changing event admissibility.
- Dissolution might genuinely open a pathway only when hard constraints affect support.
- The reported handover might conceal several distinct micro-cycles or depend on projection and normalisation.
- Different constraint families may generate a different form of ordered succession, including intermittent or branching sequences.
- The current grammar might forbid some of these behaviours; that would itself be an informative boundary.

Choose experiments to illuminate several of these possibilities together, **including behaviours not listed here**.

Guiding formulation: **orient the search toward the relational processes that might matter for gravity and temporality; do not orient the results toward a gravitational answer.**

## 3. Investigate mechanism, not just an observed pattern

The programme must separate four levels which can otherwise be conflated:

1. **Local rate-rule asymmetry:** a rule changes a formation/dissolution rate conditional on other relations.
2. **Cycle affinity / detailed-balance violation:** a closed sequence of states has a non-unit forward/reverse product of rates (Kolmogorov criterion).
3. **Steady-state probability current:** there is a nonzero oriented net flux after the stationary distribution is taken into account.
4. **Observed event-order asymmetry:** a source-relative, hub-relative or class-projected pattern is favoured in a particular event statistic.

These are not interchangeable, and a non-gradient rule is not by itself proof of a particular **observed four-step handover orientation**.

For each explored mechanism, determine where possible:

- whether any elementary two-toggle square has nonzero signed affinity;
- the orientation and magnitude of that affinity, including the exact condition under which it changes sign or vanishes;
- how local cycles combine or cancel when aggregated over states, source/probe roles or symmetry orbits;
- whether the stationary current follows that local orientation;
- whether the specific handover statistic captures the resulting currents or hides them;
- whether the apparent ordered pattern persists under a different projection or a FREE hub reference;
- whether passage/capture and temporal persistence tell a different story from steady-state occupancy.

Use symbolic derivations and exact finite-state checks where productive. A proof is valuable **if it emerges as the best explanation**, but do not organise the experiments as a search for confirming instances of a presumed theorem.

If a global theorem is proposed, state all assumptions (grammar, label covariance, factor signs, initial support, clock, recurrence class, N, boundary, action slots and projection). A finite census is evidence within its coverage, not a proof outside it. Seek counterexamples before extending the claim.

Recognise known Markov-chain mathematics—detailed balance, cycle decomposition and nonequilibrium stationary currents—without claiming these are novel physical principles. Separate a mathematical arrow in imposed transition rates from a physical thermodynamic or cosmological arrow, whose sustaining mechanism and physical bookkeeping have not been modelled here.

## 4. Explicitly examine the meaning of “transition-enabling dissolution”

This is the most important connection to the other research programme.

**Do not equate “relation A dissolved before relation B formed” with “A's dissolution enabled B.”** Temporal precedence, correlation, conditional rate modulation, increased accessibility and strict admissibility changes are different mechanisms.

For selected matched relational contexts, compare a state `x` in which the candidate supporting relation is present with an otherwise matched state `x−` obtained by removing that one relation. Use the *same* fixed rule set and declared event conventions. Record how the prospective event changes under each context.

Distinguish, at minimum:

- **Strict support enabling:** the subsequent event is prohibited in one matched state and admissible in the other. This requires an actual change in positive/zero event support; soft weights alone cannot open a forbidden event.
- **Rate enabling or suppression:** both events remain admissible, but the relative or absolute rate changes.
- **Indirect pathway enabling:** the immediate event remains available, but a multistep route, hitting probability, committor, expected passage time or conditional event-order probability changes.
- **Opportunity or denominator artefact:** the apparent preference arises from normalisation across competing events, rather than a direct change in the event's own unnormalised weight.
- **Projection/selection artefact:** the effect disappears when matched full states, support, density and available competing actions are controlled.

For direct one-step comparisons, make matched-state definitions explicit and preserve the appropriate support and action slot. For longer sequences, declare the horizon, initial-state priors and whether the analysis conditions on a preceding event or intervenes by setting a state. An imposed intervention is an *experimental instrument*, not evidence that nature contains that intervention mechanism.

Choose small, complementary contrasts inspired by CL-Q05 and the laboratory's controlled passage findings. It is acceptable for the result to be that **the present GT handover is only a kinetic sequence and not strict enabling**. That is a valuable negative finding, not a failure.

Where hard rules would be required, audit whether the existing grammar can express them without a new primitive, and assess irreducibility / absorbing classes carefully. Do not add hard constraints merely to create a desired enabling example.

## 8. Analyse results at multiple explanatory levels

Produce a synthesis separating:

**[O] Formal-system observations.** What happened in the declared finite model, with signs, magnitudes, examples, nulls, exceptions and coverage.

**[M] Mathematical mechanism.** Which rule components and cycle identities explain an observation? Which conclusions are theorems under stated assumptions, which remain empirical regularities and which fail under a counterexample?

**[RS] Ontological coherence interpretation.** How might the findings inform relational succession, persistence, coordination, directional dependence, support, release, admissibility versus tendency, or a later gravitational programme? These are interpretations, not demonstrated physics.

**[P] Physical comparison.** Only if rigorously motivated; otherwise say **none**. Do not equate a finite Markov-cycle arrow with thermodynamic entropy production in a physical substrate, gravity, physical duration or gravitational redshift.

**[T] Theological premises.** Remain distinct and outside the experimental evidential claims. No CBO text changes are authorised.

Pay particular attention to these distinctions:

- time-ordered correlation versus causally or counterfactually enabling a transition;
- a *soft* change in pathway likelihood versus a *hard* change in admissible possibilities;
- a mathematical nonequilibrium current versus a physical source of irreversibility;
- gradient occupancy/potential versus non-gradient kinetic circulation;
- succession, local activity, duration and gathering as different observables;
- a source imposed by HELD versus a reference induced by a FREE configuration;
- weak/fragile findings as potentially informative, without falsely attributing purpose or design to them.

Preserve unexpected paths that do not resemble the handover, especially if they clarify the role of release or fragility.
