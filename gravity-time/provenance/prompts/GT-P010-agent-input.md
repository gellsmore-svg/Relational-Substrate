## 4. Definitions to freeze: clocks, turnover and actual replacement

### 4.1 Episode sampling and clocks

Retain E5A's **stationary entrance-sampled (Palm)** episodes. An episode starts at `A^c -> A` and ends at the next `A -> A^c`. Explicitly distinguish it from an occupation-sampled residual lifetime.

Retain exactly E5A's **continuous-time (CT)** lifetime and **global jump-time (JT)** length. Those are known controls/re-expressions and are not three independent replications when a local clock is added.

Add the **anchor-local event count** `L_local(A)`: the mean number of events during an entrance episode that toggle a slot incident to the designated vertex `c` (for F_STAR/F_2PATH), or incident to either endpoint of the anchor edge `{a,b}` (for F_TRI), counting the exit event if it is local; the entry event is not counted. The local mask must be fixed from the frozen predicate, not selected retrospectively. Define the estimate from the stationary internal-plus-exit local flux, divided by the episode entry flux:

```
L_local(A) = [sum_{x in A} pi(x) sum_{e in LOCAL(anchor)} W(x,e)] / J_A
```

All event counts are in the **same underlying jump trajectory**. `L_local` is a count, not a physical duration or a new independent Markov time axis. It cannot itself eliminate heterogeneity at the anchor. Verify on synthetic chains and the NULL analytic control.

Report alongside `L_CT`, `L_JT`, `L_local` the local/global event share, entry frequency, motif prevalence in CT and JT, mean anchor-local hazard, and density overlap. Never relabel `L_local` as physical time.

### 4.2 The entry set and three non-equivalent kinds of change

Freeze for each entrance state `y` its **entry participants** `S0(y)`:

- `F_STAR(c)` and `F_2PATH(c)`: the neighbours of anchor `c` immediately **after entry**.
- `F_TRI(ab)`: the set of vertices that complete triangle `ab` immediately **after entry**; the anchor edge `ab` itself is a fixed part of this predicate and can never be replaced while it holds.

Track, with read-only analysis of the trajectory, at least these **distinct quantities**:

1. **Gross turnover** (`Omega`): E5A's eligible toggle count, reproduced as a check, including rapid returns.
2. **Distinct participant departures:** number/fraction of `S0` whose participation becomes absent at least once before exit. This detects changes but is not proof of replacement: a departed member may return.
3. **Strong renewal / complete replacement:** probability that **before episode exit**, a state is reached in which the anchored predicate still holds **and** *none* of `S0` currently participates (for F_TRI, none of the original completers; the anchor edge stays). The qualifying current participants must therefore come from outside `S0`. Call this `P_full_renewal`. This is stronger than “all originals left at some point” and is **not** the same as unlabelled identity or replacement of the anchor itself.

Add a predeclared flicker diagnostic, preferably exact: for example, a conditional two-step **immediate reversal** probability/flux for eligible anchor-local toggles while the episode remains active. It should be reported **separately**; do not produce an ill-defined “net Omega” by subtracting overlapping consecutive reversals. If useful and feasible, report the probability of a renewed configuration surviving through at least one further local transition without restoring any original participant; define any such secondary filter **before** scientific execution, and leave it unresolved if its cost is excessive.

### 4.3 Structural feasibility is part of the result

A zero probability is not a dynamical negative when complete replacement is **combinatorially impossible**.

For a vertex-degree predicate `deg(c) >= k`, complete simultaneous disjointness from `S0` while maintaining the predicate requires at least `k` potential partners **outside** `S0`, i.e. `N-1-|S0| >= k`.

- `F_STAR`, `k=3`, has *no* feasible complete renewal at **either N=5 or N=6**: an entrance episode crosses from degree 2 to degree 3 and there are respectively 1 or 2 other potential neighbours, not 3. The proper result is `STRUCTURALLY_IMPOSSIBLE`, **not** evidence that the rules fail to maintain the star.
- `F_2PATH`, `k=2`, can be feasible at both sizes for degree-2 entry sets; check actual entrance classes.
- `F_TRI` feasibility depends on its entry completer set and remaining available completers; compute it from the declared anchor and N, including entrances due to formation of the anchor edge.

For every predicate/family/N report: the fraction of entry episodes in feasible entry classes, full-renewal probability **conditional on feasibility**, and unconditional full-renewal probability, clearly distinguished. Where feasibility is zero, report the structural theorem, **not** an estimated zero. Also report maximum feasible partial renewal in F_STAR, so this family remains informative without a fabricated total-replacement requirement.

### 4.4 Computation and identification

Prefer **exact finite-generator** functionals, not stochastic trajectories as the primary evidence. An augmented state tracking `S0` for the duration of an episode is a **read-only computational observer**, not a new state read by the stochastic rules. Define boundary conditions for exit-before-renewal and solve hitting/committor systems, ideally quotienting by permutations that preserve the anchor and entry set. Check a small-N enumerated fixture against an independent trajectory enumerator or two methods. Preserve exact definition of entrance weights and tie handling.

If the augmented solve is not reliable/feasible at N=6 within the declared budget, mark the channel `UNRESOLVED` with counts, limits and diagnostics; do not substitute a weaker statistic under the same name. Keep every frozen cell and its status in the output.

## 5. Comparator family — strengthen the controls without inventing a matched mechanism

Compare all target readouts against **three clearly distinct references**, with every comparator algorithm and rate fixed before executing E5B.

### 5.1 N0 and ND (continuity)

1. **N0:** the ordinary unit-rate NULL process at the same N (baseline correctness control).
2. **ND:** exactly the E5A density-profile and mean-CT-rate matched reversible process from `e5-definitions-and-null-construction.md` T6. Do not silently change it. It matches the CT edge-count marginal `p_k` and `E_pi[q]`, but usually not the jump density or placement of activity. Retain PCOMP as the known kinetic false-attractor control.

### 5.2 New predeclared kinetic projection control: NK

Construct a **density- and density-layer-flux-matched exchangeable Markov surrogate** of each target. This improves on ND by matching the *stationary birth/death traffic between adjacent edge-count layers*, not merely a single average event rate. Call it **NK** in this prompt (rename only before freeze and record alias if the code already has a naming convention).

Let `M = C(N,2)`, `k = |x|`, and let `p_k = sum_{|x|=k} pi(x)` be the target CT density profile. For every adjacent layer, define the target's **stationary upward flux**:

```
J[k,k+1] = sum_{|x|=k} pi(x) * sum_{e absent in x} W(x,e)
```

By stationarity this equals the target's downward flux from k+1 to k. For a comparator state with k present edges, use the same birth rate for every absent edge and the same death rate for every present edge:

```
b_k     = J[k,k+1] / ((M-k) * p_k)      for 0 <= k < M
d_(k+1) = J[k,k+1] / ((k+1) * p_(k+1))  for 0 <= k < M
```

Then the proposed comparator:

- is label-covariant and reversible by layerwise detailed balance;
- has stationary CT law `pi_NK(x) = p_|x| / C(M,|x|)`;
- matches **every adjacent-layer stationary flux** and hence the layer-conditioned mean **total jump rate** `E[q | k]`;
- also matches the overall event rate and **JT density profile** (unlike ND), provided those identities hold numerically;
- **does not** match within-layer arrangement of rates, anchor-local activity conditional on configuration, shape correlations or future multi-time structure. It is a control for the declared projection, **not** a universal “fair kinetics” counterfactual.

Prove these statements as small propositions and test them against an independent calculation of `p_k`, bidirectional flux and jump-density profile. Calculate rates from **positive flux sums**, not numerically fragile subtraction of nearly equal terms. Report rare layers, tiny rates, conditioning and solver uncertainty; never smooth zero or rare bins post hoc. If this comparator cannot be implemented in the stated numerical regime, leave its channels unresolved rather than manufacturing a substitute.

**Important:** NK uses target generator/stationary data to define a **projection**, but the **formula and diagnostics must be frozen before those data are applied to the new E5B quantities**. This is an adaptive, declared comparison, not blind confirmation.
