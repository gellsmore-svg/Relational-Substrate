## 0. Read this first: what the human researcher wants

We are developing a relational-substrate ontology and testing some of its mathematical possibilities, not constructing an imitation of general relativity. The human researcher is the sole human researcher; ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Codex are computational/research assistants, **not a human research team**.

The work has **two complementary, recursively informing lines**:

1. **Bottom-up foundational inquiry:** Begin with stochastic relational actualisation and constraints. Discover which organisation, persistence, orientation, recovery, relational distinction, reference, or other capabilities can be generated; distinguish primitives, mathematical mechanisms, artifacts, and genuine emergent behaviour.
2. **Top-down coherence/gap inquiry:** Use gravitational, temporal, spatial and eventually motion-like phenomena to illuminate what explanatory capabilities would still have to be built between the mathematical substrate and anything physically interpretable. The top-down perspective *guides information-seeking*; it is not a demand that an experiment look gravitational.

The direction of work may move freely between these lines. A foundational detour is successful if it reveals an essential mechanism, closes or exposes a conceptual gap, or changes how an existing result should be interpreted. Do not artificially steer every round toward positive attraction. Do not declare that mathematical directionality is physical time, that an attachment preference is physical attraction, or that changing adjacency constitutes acceleration.

**The immediate reason for GT-P007 is an important E3 finding:** the held triangle used as a reference in earlier experiments contributes an exact, state-independent internal path-witness factor to every source–probe attachment rate. In E3B, removing this factor *with a non-covariant counterfactual instrument* led positive-σ path families to display attachment selectivity above the null while retaining their directional handover. This is scientifically suggestive **but is not yet an ordinary, label-covariant experiment with unchanged grammar**.

Your task is to determine what actually happens when the held reference has different internal structure but **the same unmodified relational rules**. Negative, mixed, fragile, null, or unexpected results are all legitimate outcomes.

### Non-negotiable epistemic distinctions

- A **held scaffold** is an experimental boundary condition, *not* an emergent object.
- A **label-covariant rule** must read relational incidence, not special named source vertices.
- A **measurement** may name the held source *as an instrument*, but that label must not affect actual transition rates.
- A **source-dependent occupancy preference** is not necessarily drift, approach, force, or acceleration.
- A **counterfactual E3B neutralisation** is not a native rule and does not independently demonstrate gathering.
- The governing outcome is increased understanding and coherence, **not confirming the prior E3B interpretation**.

## 2. Scientific question, without a required outcome

**What aspects of source–probe gathering, directional succession, local activity, persistence and recovery are consequences of the held reference's actual internal relational structure, and what aspects belong to the unmodified transition rules?**

Use multiple incomplete possibilities, including:

- **P1 — Triangle-specific inhibition:** the old failure to gather is largely caused by the triangle's fixed internal two-path witnesses. A source without such witnesses may show source–probe selectivity with the same path constraints.
- **P2 — Source size / combinatorics:** differences follow the number of source vertices, free relation slots, probes, available attachment slots, or source share—not specifically internal witness topology.
- **P3 — Source topology beyond the witness floor:** different held structures change *state-dependent* path, closure and adjacency witnesses, occupancy and kinetic competition, so removing the triangle's fixed floor alone is an incomplete explanation.
- **P4 — Clock/density artifact:** apparent gathering may be positive only in the raw per-event margin, at sparse densities, or in rare conditional states; a physically suggestive reading would not survive ordinary comparison controls.
- **P5 — Structure-sensitive dynamics:** some source geometries alter directional handover, recovery or local activity even when the signed local affinity formula remains applicable.
- **P6 — No general gathering:** even without the triangle's witness floor, the rule families remain non-gathering under fair native and size-matched comparisons. That would be a valuable negative result.
- **P7 — Something unexpected:** find, retain and explain previously unnamed behaviour or equivalence.

The mathematical local result `A = σ·(deg(r) − deg(q))` concerns intact elementary squares with positive factors. It does **not** guarantee the sign of aggregated circulation or source-relative handovers in every scaffold.

Do not select a grade, topology, metric or analysis threshold after seeing results to make an attractive margin positive. A true null or negative result should be retained without a rescue sweep.

## 3. Authorised engineering: a configurable, scientifically neutral held scaffold

Implement the smallest backwards-compatible facility to specify **which existing pair relations are held present**, with an optional derived source vertex set for measurements. This is an experimental boundary-condition change, **not a new grammar primitive**.

### 3.1 Essential semantics

- Held edges are permanently present and non-dissolvable; their rates are suppressed only as required by the held-boundary semantics already used for the triangle.
- All other edge slots retain exactly the original formation/dissolution grammar, including non-held pairs whose endpoints both belong to a designated source. Do **not** freeze absent source-internal edges unless separately specified and justified in a new scientific plan.
- The source set for connected held graphs is the set of vertices incident on the held edges. All other vertices are probes. If a disconnected reference is ever considered, it must have separately declared measurement semantics; do not include such a cell by default.
- The **constraints themselves cannot consult source labels, scaffold membership, or manually specified witness corrections**. They can read the full graph, including held edges, through the existing covariant templates. An observer may refer to the held configuration when classifying source/probe pairs.
- Permuting vertex labels while permuting the held graph must preserve the kernel up to the same permutation. Do not hardcode vertex numbers into scientific rules.
- Preserve a legacy default of the original held triangle; its transition weights and published observables must reproduce previous experiments on a bitwise or rigorously justified numerical basis.
- Prefer generic representation of a held edge set rather than special-casing `EDGE`, `PATH`, and `TRIANGLE` throughout the code.
- Keep the source shape explicitly in plan IDs, result IDs, reproducibility hashes and atlas entries. Do not let different topologies silently share cached kernels or results.

### 3.2 Minimum source structures

The first scientific round should consider three related but non-equivalent experimental references:

**S1. Held single edge (`K2`).** Two connected source vertices, one fixed edge; **no internal held two-path**. The remaining vertices are probes.

**S2. Held three-vertex open path (`P3`).** Two fixed edges on three source vertices; the closing chord remains a *free* relation, which may form or dissolve under the ordinary rules. The path's endpoints and middle have different fixed internal witness counts. This is the most useful comparison against the triangle because the source vertex count stays at three.

**S3. Held triangle (`K3`).** Three source vertices, three fixed edges; the original experimental reference with two fixed internal two-path witnesses for every attachment.

Do not describe P3 as having **no** internal two-paths. It has one fixed two-path from each endpoint. Its unheld source chord is dynamically variable, and closing that chord can alter witness counts further. That is a scientifically valuable discriminator, not an implementation nuisance.

### 3.3 Analytical witness census before execution

Derive and test, directly from the grammar's ordered witness multiplicities, the number of **wholly internal fixed-source path witnesses** on each source–probe attachment slot:

- K2: `0` at both source vertices;
- P3: `1` at each endpoint and `0` at the middle, **before additional state-dependent witnesses**;
- K3: `2` at each source vertex.

For each source type, separate:

1. witnesses supplied entirely by held edges (permanent floor);
2. witnesses supplied by non-held source-internal edges (particularly the P3 chord);
3. witnesses involving probes or other non-held edges;
4. witness counts for probe–probe and non-held source–source slots.

The analytic fixed floor is a **mechanism explanation and cross-check**, never a correction silently subtracted from the native kernel.

Compute and report the exact source-size and slot-count null geometry. For N entities, K held edges, k source vertices and N−k probes:

- total pair slots `C(N,2)`;
- free slots `M = C(N,2) − K`;
- possible source–probe attachment slots `k(N−k)`;
- probe–probe slots `C(N−k,2)`;
- non-held source-internal slots `C(k,2) − K`;
- under the uniform free-edge NULL model, the attachment fraction conditional on a nonempty free-edge set has a geometry-specific baseline `r0 = k(N−k)/M` (validate against actual estimator semantics).

**Illustrative NULL baselines (verify independently):**

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5   | K2  | 1   | 9   | 6   | 6/9 |

These baselines **cannot be interchanged** between scaffold types. Notice that comparing K2 to K3 also changes source vertex count, whereas comparing P3 to K3 holds it fixed but changes the held-edge count and leaves a dynamic chord.

## 4. E4A: bounded, informative native-scaffold comparisons

E4A is the **first independent native/covariant test of the E3B scaffold explanation**. It is not a confirmatory experiment about physical gravity.

### 4.1 Suggested complementary design

Prefer a coherent matrix at N=5 and N=6 across K2, P3 and K3 references, with the same originally defined W4 grades and these families:

1. `NULL` — geometry-specific baseline and reversible identity.
2. `PATHF weak_suppress` — positive σ, path formation suppression.
3. `PATHH weak_favour` — positive σ, path dissolution favour.
4. `CSPH strong_favour+weak_favour` — E0B/E1 closure-plus-path coexistence candidate.
5. `PCOMP weak_favour+weak_favour` — σ=0, different kinetics and a useful clock/null control.
6. `CLOSEW weak_favour` — reversible, closure-based gradient gathering control.

This would be **36 cells** for 3 scaffolds × 2 sizes × 6 families, *including published triangle counterparts as labelled baseline replications rather than novel discoveries*. You may substitute or reduce cells **before freezing** if measured feasibility demands it, but preserve:

- at least one clean positive-σ formation-acting and one dissolution-acting path family;
- a matched NULL for every scaffold/size;
- at least one reversible gradient or σ=0 comparator;
- **P3 versus K3 at equal source vertex count**;
- at least one K2 comparison to eliminate the fixed internal path floor;
- ideally at least one cross-size check that can reveal changing geometry.

A well-explained smaller matrix is better than many low-information cells. Do not use a large uncontrolled alphabet or topology sweep. Additional opposite-sign families or source sizes are possible *candidates for E4B*, not automatic E4A additions.

### 4.2 Measurements (all declared before execution)

For every cell, report under both the **continuous-time** and **jump/event** conventions where well-defined:

- source–probe attachment fraction, the correct size-and-topology-specific NULL difference, and its ceiling-normalised value;
- free-edge count/density distribution, and attachment conditional on number of free edges, with clear handling of zero edges;
- density-standardised attachment comparison against that topology's NULL profile, alongside the supported density range, overlap diagnostics and rare-mass flags; never promote an extrapolated conditional to a robust finding;
- number and fraction of detached probes, number of source-attached probes and distribution of attachment counts;
- endpoint/middle attachment heterogeneity in P3, normalised against the number of eligible slots; distinguish scaffold orbits from arbitrary label effects;
- dynamic occupancy of the P3 closing chord and its conditional effect on witness counts, attachment and activity;
- event-order handover/partner frame and irreversible flux; compare orientation and magnitude, not merely presence of nonzero affinity;
- local activity / recurrence near each source orbit, with clock definitions; sources are held, so do not mistake held-edge inactivity for probe clock slowing;
- available persistence/recovery or attachment autocorrelation measurements, only where computationally feasible, with their clock or event units;
- saturation, absorbing or disconnected recurrent structure, numerical residuals, symmetry and support warnings.

Preserve all weak, null, reversed or unexpected outcomes. Do not describe a preferential **occupancy** of attachment states as directed physical approach.

### 4.3 Predeclared discriminators

Freeze these *as questions with conditional readings*, not as predetermined positive thresholds:

- Does the native **K2** scaffold generate above-NULL source–probe selectivity for a positive-σ path family *without modifying any rule rate using source membership*?
- Does **P3** exhibit an intermediate or qualitatively different effect relative to K2 and K3? Does its *middle vs endpoints* behave as predicted by the actual internal-witness census, including dynamic chord closure?
- Does P3→K3 at constant number of source vertices follow the permanent-witness mechanism, or do density and support/kinetic changes dominate?
- Is gathering consistent across clocks, occupancy conditions and non-rare density regions, or dependent on measurement convention?
- Does the same family show *both* source-selective occupancy and oriented succession in the native grammar, and does one change independently of the other?
- Does removing the triangle-specific floor explain the **sign**, but not necessarily the magnitude, of native scaffold differences? Do not expect the native K2 law to equal the E3B neutralised K3 law: their source sizes, free slots and full witness structures differ.
- Do the gradient and σ=0 controls behave according to their own mathematics? When a control fails, investigate before interpreting target findings.
- Which observations are genuine structural changes versus null-geometry normalization, sample density, source participation or observer frame?

**Do not freeze a statement such as “K2 must gather”.** Freezing means fixing *what to measure and how*, so an unexpected negative result is equally valuable.

### 4.4 Budget, feasibility and stopping

The number of unconstrained free slots varies strongly by scaffold. In particular HELD N=6 K2 has `2^14 = 16,384` possible states, P3 has `2^13 = 8,192`, and K3 has `2^12 = 4,096`; use measured memory/runtime rather than assuming the old triangle timing applies.

Suggested E4A limits: **up to 3 hours round wall-time**, max 4 workers and an explicit memory ceiling based on host capacity; per-cell watchdog and numerical acceptance must be frozen before scientific execution. If forecasts exceed resources, downselect *before freezing* without favouring any expected result. Incomplete cells stay incomplete, with reasons; do not retune tolerances after looking at results.

The code must enforce the frozen plan's commit, hashes and predeclared cell matrix, and abort on contract drift.
