## Deviations and notes, in order

1. **Pre-freeze guard.** In `gte3b` a normalised-margin guard (None when r0 ≥ 1, N=4 only) was added before freezing. This is the same rule as in `gte1`.
2. **E3A: FREE N=5 W16 PFD strong+strong.**
   - The GTH referee's continuous law differs by 2.6e-10 L1, against a tolerance of 1e-10. The law is exactly uniform by theorem, and S3 holds at 1e-9. The stationary solver needed 861,000 iterations: this cell mixes very slowly.
   - The fixed-point recovery reference did not converge within its 200,000-step cap, since recovery times reach 17,800 events.
   - **Post hoc** (not frozen): the linear recovery solves have a relative residual of 1.5e-15.
   - The cell stays flagged numerically qualified.
3. **E3A: HELD N=5 W16 PF strong.** The recovery reference differs by 7.2e-8 on values near 270 (3e-10 relative), against a frozen tolerance that was absolute (1e-8). **Post hoc**: the solve residual is 8e-16. The cell stays flagged. The design flaw (absolute instead of relative tolerance) is mine.
4. **E3A atlas command.** The atlas is generated by `python -m rs_constraint_lab.gte3a atlas` and the E3B atlas by `python -m rs_constraint_lab.gte3b atlas`. The batch CLI wrappers do not expose `atlas`.
5. **E3B check K (kernel unchanged off attachment slots)** failed in all 18 cells with deviation exactly 1.0. The check divided by the zero weights of held source slots. **Post hoc**: a direct comparison shows the kernels identical off attachment slots (largest difference 0 over 18 cells). The frozen result is recorded as failed.
6. **E3B check C2 (PCOMP unchanged)** failed (largest channel change 0.095 at N=5). The expectation was wrong. The floor scales PCOMP's attachment formation and dissolution equally, so the continuous-time law is unchanged, but attachments' total rates change, so jump-clock channels move.
7. **Relation types.** GT-P006 suggests `not_implied_by`, `replaces_explanation` and `requires_definition`. The shared ledger vocabulary (`constraint-lab/src/rs_constraint_lab/coherence.py`) does not include them, and that module is shared with the laboratory, so it was not edited. They were mapped to `undermines` / `contrasts_with`, `supersedes_interpretation` and `raises_question`.
8. **The E3B instrument is not a grammar rule.** It reads scaffold membership and is not label-covariant. It is labelled as such in the plan, tables, atlas, report and ledger.
