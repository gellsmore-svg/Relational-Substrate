## 2. Structurally derived (not inserted) — the wins

These fell out of *one* ontological choice (nematic RP², 3D), not from fitting:

- **Fermionic matter with spin-½** — the single most important result for reaching
  atoms; from the substrate's grain, via FR (cross-checked by Wilczek–Zee `θ=π`).
- **Charge quantised and sign-locked to spin** — charge is an integer topological
  number; its sign is not free but gauged by the spin frame.
- **Magnetism and electricity as one split identity** — not two forces; twist +
  writhe of one conserved knot, with **induction** as their exchange, and polarity
  as twist handedness. This *is* the book's E&M-complementary claim, in equations.
- **A discrete species lattice** — a candidate particle inventory, `H` a quadratic
  form on charge quanta.
- **Suggestive** (flagged, not asserted): no magnetic monopole (integer charge vs
  continuous twist); charge conjugation ↔ spin Z₂; merons ↔ constituent structure.

## 3. Chosen, not forced — the load-bearing assumptions

Honesty requires naming what could be wrong:

- **The nematic RP² / 3D-bulk substrate** (D7–D8). Motivated (spin-½, gentler on
  background-independence, = AMS magnetic geometry) but *chosen*. Its falsifier is
  explicit: if the vorton's 2π rotation were the trivial π₁(Q) element, no
  fermions.
- **Darkness = a director at rest.** A definite ontological posit (D6).
- **Background-independence not yet fully secured** — whether only textures (not
  the absolute rest axis) are observable is still open.

## 4. Not yet addressed

- **Any quantitative anchor.** Everything so far is *structure, sign, and
  discreteness* — deliberately (invariants-over-magnitudes, this phase). No number
  has been reproduced yet. This is the honest ceiling.
- **EM coupling** (next).
- **Assembly into atoms/molecules** (secondary topology; scaffold T6) and
  **light–vorton admissibility** (T7).

## 5. Confidence — two axes, kept separate

Following the project's lens-vs-law discipline:

- **Internal coherence / containment — rising.** One choice forced a long,
  self-consistent cascade with several correlations to known physics motifs
  (spin-statistics, induction, charge quantisation, emergent EM). The containment
  ledger has tightened materially. This is real and earned.
- **Empirical correctness — low, capped.** No parameter-free quantitative
  reproduction yet; the central substrate choice is motivated-but-unproven. We do
  **not** claim this is correct physics — only a coherent, could-fail structural
  program that has not yet failed.

The two must not be conflated. Tightening containment ≠ confirming physics.

## 6. Measured against the north star

North star: atoms, molecules, light, E&M from one substrate where the vorton is
the sole structural primitive.

- **Constituents: substantially in place.** A charged, spin-½, fermionic vorton,
  with E&M as its own twist/writhe — the *ingredients* of atoms are structurally
  derived, not inserted.
- **Assembly: not yet.** Atoms need the species spectrum pinned *and* binding
  (secondary topology) *and* EM coupling. That is the far half of the road.

Roughly: the **constituent layer** is drafted and internally coherent; the
**assembly layer** is untouched.

## 7. Anti-drift watch

The EM-coupling step introduces parameters (stiffnesses, a Hopf/WZ coefficient).
**These must not be calibrated to hit targets** — that was the prior cycle's exact
failure (D1, D4). The test there stays *structural* (does charge = emergent flux?
does the emergent potential obey Maxwell?), never a numeric fit.
