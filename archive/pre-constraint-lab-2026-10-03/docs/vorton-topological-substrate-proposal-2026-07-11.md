## 0. Why this document exists (the drift finding)

The prior modelling core (`src/model.js` + the resilience/regime layer) is
**retired as the programme's spine**, for a reason the project's own record
already states plainly:

- Against static physical observables (refractive index, molecular geometry,
  peroxide torsion) *"the grammar is **calibrated**, not used to derive a
  prediction. Result: consistency at best, never novel evidence"*
  (`relational-substrate-validation-status.md`).
- The refractive-index success *"is the established Shannon–Fischer 2016 method,
  so the pass is consistency with established crystal chemistry, not independent
  evidence."*
- From there the programme **pivoted to a path-dependent order effect** (gentle-
  first "coaxing" resilience) and pursued it across metal fatigue, cardiac
  preconditioning, wildfire, and stress-psychology.

That pivot is **objective drift**. The stated outcome was *a mathematically
plausible substrate model of atoms, molecules, light, electricity and
magnetism*. The stress-history / resilience programme optimised instead for *a
novel prediction that beats a statistical null* — a different objective the model
substituted for the stated one. The order-effect work is therefore declared
**out of scope** here. It is archived, not deleted, and may be revisited only as
an incidental downstream effect, never as the north star.

The diagnosis of *why* the old core could only ever calibrate: it is a
first-order **linear-response** form — coherence is an affine blend of the five
grammar sliders, and all history/memory enters as ±3–12% multiplicative nudges.
Such a form recovers the *direction* of effects and never their *magnitude* or
*structure*. It is the wrong mathematics for a substrate whose primitives are
**topological**.

## 1. The locked objective (north star — the anti-drift anchor)

> **A single relational substrate in which the *vorton* (primary topology) is the
> sole structural primitive, and matter (secondary topology), light, electricity
> and magnetism are its downstream expressions — with electricity and magnetism
> treated as complementary modes of one substrate, not as independent forces.**

Three commitments make this an *anchor* rather than a slogan:

1. **Only the vorton is structural.** Per the ontology, *light = Operation A
   (propagation)*, *electricity = Operation B (reconfiguration)*, *magnetism =
   Operation C (alignment)*, *vorton = Operation D (closure formation)*. Of these
   only D yields a stable identity-bearing structure. Charge is explicitly *"a
   directional bias… derivative in explanatory status, real in consequence, **and
   not a substance**."* Any model that promotes a field description (E, B, a
   bivector) to structural rank has **drifted** and must be corrected. *(This
   corrects an earlier suggestion in the design discussion to anchor on Geometric
   Algebra's E+IB bivector — that inverts the hierarchy: it makes E&M structural,
   which the ontology forbids.)*
2. **Success = structural reproduction of known physics from one substrate**, not
   novelty. Re-deriving an established quantity *parameter-free from the vorton*
   is a win, not the dead end the prior cycle judged it to be.
3. **Ontology before model** (the books' own discipline). The mathematics below is
   a *language and instantiation* for the vorton; it does not reduce the ontology
   to itself.

## 2. The corrected substrate anchor: topological-soliton / knot-field theory

The vorton is defined as *"a stable torsional knot-state of the relational
substrate… identity carried topologically rather than materially,"* with
invariants that are *"closure class, winding relations, linking structure… a
stability envelope."* The mathematics that speaks exactly this language is
**topological-soliton theory**, and its lineage is the vorton's own:

- **Kelvin's vortex atom (1867)** — atoms as knotted vortex tubes in a continuous
  medium: the literal ancestor of "vorton," and the seed of both knot theory and
  modern solitons.
- **Skyrme model** — particles (baryons) as **topological solitons of a
  continuous field**, classified by an *integer* topological charge. Precedent
  that particle properties can be *derived* from a knot in a continuum.
- **Hopfions** — closed, twisted, **toroidal** solitons whose identity *is* an
  integer **Hopf / linking number**, *topologically protected under continuous
  deformation* (= the book's **stability envelope**), now realised experimentally
  in magnets and in light.

**The ontological rank is preserved, not dissolved.** The technical volume
already refuses to reduce the vorton to a soliton ("*ontologically stronger… not
merely any persistent pattern*"). That stands: topological-soliton theory
supplies the vorton's **equations and invariants**; RS supplies the claim that
this knot-state is *primary* and that matter is constituted from it.

**Where Geometric Algebra re-enters — one tier down.** GA is a *local* language
of rotation and orientation. It is the wrong tool for the *structure* (a global,
topological fact) but the right tool for the *downstream derivation*: once a
vorton exists, GA is the natural language for computing how its circulation
*expresses* as Operations A/B/C, and how a transient (light) interacts with it.
So the split is:

| Half of the north star | Mathematics |
|---|---|
| The vorton *structure* (Operation D output) | topological-soliton / knot-field theory (Kelvin → Skyrme → Hopfion) |
| The *downstream* lanes (light A, electricity B, magnetism C) from the vorton's circulation | Geometric Algebra, layered on top |

## 3. The RS ↔ mathematics mapping (faithful to both books)

A hopfion carries **two linked circulations** (toroidal and poloidal). RS already
names E and M as **complementary** modes — the book's resonance chapter pairs
*capacitive (stored disequilibrium, Op B)* with *inductive (held alignment, Op
C)*. The two linked circulations are the natural structural home for that
complementarity:

| RS ontology | Book wording | Topological-soliton reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Vorton** (primary topology, Op D) | "stable torsional knot-state… identity carried topologically" | a **hopfion-class** soliton; identity = **Hopf/linking invariant** + stability envelope |
| **Magnetism** (Op C) | *"static torsional equilibrium — a held twist-geometry"*; polarity = *"opposite chirality of the same held configuration"*; dissipates no energy (held geometry, not activity) | the **held twist** of the soliton; **polarity = sign/handedness of the linking number** (a static topological chirality — hence no dissipation) |
| **Electricity** (Op B) | reconfiguration; voltage = torsional disequilibrium; current = coherent reseating of a vorton lattice; charge = derivative asymmetry | the **dynamic reconfiguration/slip** mode; **charge magnitude = the integer topological charge** |
| **E & M complementary** | capacitive ↔ inductive resonance between "complementary forms of ordered constraint" | the two **linked circulations** (dynamic flow ↔ static held twist) of one soliton |
| **Light** (Op A) | transient, "open, coherent, route-bearing, unwindable" | a **non-topological** propagating excitation of the same substrate; light–vorton coupling = the existing **admissibility** walkthroughs (salvaged) |
| **Matter** (secondary topology) | lattices/interfaces from coupled vortons | multi-soliton **coupling** regimes; molecules = bound hopfion complexes |

**The sandbox already gropes toward this.** `closedForms = ['circle','trefoil',
'double']` are knot/link classes (unknot, trefoil, link); the six identity
metrics (closure/return/boundedness/coherence/reseating/leakage) are *heuristic
stand-ins* for a topological invariant. The core upgrade is to **replace the
hand-tuned identity-score with a genuine invariant (Hopf / linking number)** —
the same move that ends the "calibrated, not derived" problem.

## 4. Anti-drift discipline (carried over, sharpened)

- **Parameter-free or it doesn't count.** The failure mode to avoid is per-case
  calibration. A prediction that needs a fitted coefficient is not a derivation.
- **Pre-register every test** (success *and* failure criteria) before running it —
  the one genuinely valuable habit inherited from the prior cycle.
- **Structural reproduction of known physics is the objective**, not a novel
  null-beating effect. Reproducing charge quantisation from a linking number is a
  *win*.
- **Confidence stays capped** until a parameter-free quantitative reproduction
  lands in a domain no established theory already covers, or a known quantity is
  reproduced with genuinely no free handles.
- **The data-access wall is real** (numerically-extractable held-out data is
  scarce) and is a first-class project risk, not an excuse.

## 5. First pre-registered could-fail test

**Claim under test:** *charge is quantised because it is a topological (linking)
invariant of the vorton — an integer — and magnetic polarity is the sign of that
same invariant.*

- **Setup:** instantiate a vorton as a hopfion-class configuration of a single
  continuous substrate field; compute its **Hopf/linking invariant** directly
  from the configuration (no fitted parameter).
- **Prediction A (charge):** admissible stable configurations carry **integer**
  topological charge; there is no stable fractional-charge vorton in the base
  species. Success = discreteness falls out of the topology, not a threshold we
  tuned.
- **Prediction B (polarity):** reversing the **handedness** of the held twist
  flips the sign of the invariant and nothing else — reproducing *"north and
  south as opposite chirality of the same held configuration"* with **no
  additional structure**.
- **Precedent that this is not fantasy:** the Skyrme model already yields integer
  baryon number as a topological charge.
- **Failure criterion (stated in advance):** if stable configurations require a
  tuned parameter to land on integers, or if polarity reversal demands structure
  beyond a sign flip of the invariant, the mapping is falsified at the first rung
  and we say so.

Only if this rung holds do we climb: two coupled vortons → a bond quantity;
light (transient) admissibility against a vorton of known invariant; then a
secondary-topology (molecule) quantity — each pre-registered, each parameter-free.

## 7. Open questions (honest frontier)

1. **Species spectrum.** Does a *single* substrate field admit a hopfion spectrum
   whose invariants match a recognisable particle inventory — or only one species?
2. **Continuum → known constants.** Can any established constant (a mass or charge
   *ratio*, a g-factor) emerge parameter-free, or does the substrate need
   additional structure (and if so, is that structure still "one substrate")?
3. **Molecules as secondary topology.** Is there *any* bond quantity that coupled
   hopfions reproduce without calibration? (This is where the prior cycle only
   ever calibrated.)
4. **What GA actually buys.** Does layering GA on top yield the E/M expressions
   *derivationally*, or is it re-description?
