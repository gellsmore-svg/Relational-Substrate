# Relational Substrate

**A global coherence research programme.** The Relational Substrate (RS) project asks whether the physical world, living organisation, persons, moral responsibility, and the biblical account of creation, corruption and restoration can be understood as belonging to **one coherent reality—without reducing them to one kind of thing**.

Physics is indispensable to that inquiry, but it is not its boundary. The project examines what physical theories describe, what they leave open about reality itself, and how proposals about the foundations of matter relate to other kinds of reality. Its guiding question is not simply *Can a model reproduce known physics?* but **What account of reality holds together most coherently across all the domains it must accommodate?**

**The visible rests upon what does not appear.** The project takes Hebrews 11:3 (KJV)—“things which are seen were not made of things which do appear”—as a governing scriptural premise, not as an unresolved question awaiting a physical experiment. It does not claim that Scripture specifies the architecture of that unseen grounding. Its central *ontological hypothesis* is that material individuality is constituted and sustained within a deeper, **created relational order**, rather than beginning with self-contained material objects that acquire relationships afterwards. The project calls this proposed order the **Relational Substrate**. **Matter is fully real. What remains open is how its material individuality is constituted from that deeper order**, what the relevant relations are, and whether the substrate itself is spatial. The scriptural premise, the particular relational interpretation, and any proposed physical mechanism are distinct claims.

The enquiry is developed from an explicitly **Scripture-first Christian perspective**. Scripture sets theological commitments and boundaries; it does not specify a substrate, an equation, or a mechanism of physical interaction. Empirical observations and successful mathematical physics constrain physical proposals. The project's philosophical arguments, experimental models, and theological premises are kept identifiable so readers can assess them separately.

## What global coherence means

A globally coherent explanation must do more than sound plausible in one discipline or collect attractive analogies between several. It should:

- **Preserve what is known:** accommodate observation, measurement, established mathematical results, and the actual claims made by Scripture within their respective domains.
- **Connect without conflating:** explain how different domains can belong to one order without treating physical processes, life, consciousness, moral obligation, and spiritual reality as interchangeable.
- **Maintain definitions and explanatory responsibilities:** carry concepts across disciplinary boundaries without silently changing their meaning, adding hidden assumptions, or exempting a preferred theory from burdens imposed on competitors.
- **Survive a round trip:** remain consistent when an explanation moves from one domain to another and back, including where a proposed bridge fails.
- **Remain revisable:** retain alternatives, contradictions, negative findings, and open questions rather than treating conceptual elegance as proof.

Coherence is a criterion for evaluating accounts of reality, **not by itself a conclusive demonstration of a particular account**. Structural resemblance does not establish shared mechanism; a mathematical model does not validate an ontology merely by behaving interestingly; and a theological interpretation is not an experimental observation.

**Ultimate ontology is not settled by demanding experimental proof of an origin or an unseen foundation.** Observation and mathematics provide powerful constraints on proposed explanations, but successful predictions do not uniquely identify the underlying reality. Quantum theory is extraordinarily accurate within its domain while allowing sharply different ontological interpretations. That motivates serious inquiry into what lies beneath the description; it does **not**, on its own, identify the Relational Substrate. Nor does the absence of a uniquely decisive experiment mean that every conceivable ontology deserves equal consideration. The project evaluates proposals through scriptural commitments, logical coherence, explanatory economy, agreement with established knowledge, comparative explanatory burdens, and any discriminating consequences that can actually be tested. Its conclusions can be argued for without pretending they amount to an absolute proof of how reality originated.

## The present conceptual framework

The current account is developed in [*Coherent Biblical Ontology*, second edition](books/coherent-biblical-ontology-second-edition.md), especially its [Current Ontological Reference (Appendix E)](books/v2/manuscript/21-appendix-e-ontology.md). The principal ideas, with their status kept explicit, include:

| Question | Working approach |
| --- | --- |
| **What is fundamental?** | On the scriptural premise that the seen does not originate in what appears (Hebrews 11:3), the RS **working hypothesis** is that real material individuals are constituted as stable, persistent forms within a deeper created relational order that is not itself material. Material-first and cofundamental accounts are examined as comparative alternatives; **how relational constitution occurs** remains open. |
| **What can happen?** | Distinguish **admissibility** (which configurations or transitions are possible) from **tendency** (how admissible outcomes are weighted). **T0** is a minimal label for whatever conditions actualisation, not a declared realm or substance. |
| **What actually happens?** | Explore **constrained stochastic actualisation**: genuine openness within structured possibility. Fundamental indeterminacy is a working hypothesis, not a consequence proved by quantum experiments or stochastic simulations. |
| **What persists?** | Examine identity through **relational invariants, consequential equivalence, provenance, and lineage**. Sameness of kind or function is not automatically continuity of the same individual. |
| **How does one distinction matter to another?** | **Transmit–Carry–Receive (T-C-R)** is the proposed grammar of consequential manifestation: **distinction → preserved dependency → consequence**. It is not yet a demonstrated universal physical mechanism. |
| **How do complex wholes arise?** | Investigate organisation, compatible reception, recursive higher-order identity, and constraints exerted by wholes upon their components—without assuming that every organised pattern constitutes a new ontological category. |
| **How do different kinds of reality belong together?** | Seek continuity of coherent relations across physical, living, personal and moral life, while preserving their genuinely different kinds of explanation and the Creator–creature distinction. |

The book distinguishes **what is observed**, **what is mathematically established within a specified model**, **what is philosophically inferred**, **what is proposed as an RS hypothesis**, and **what is held on scriptural grounds**. These are not interchangeable forms of support.

## How the inquiry is conducted

This is not merely a literature-synthesis project. Three intellectual activities interact:

1. **Identify existing knowledge.** Locate relevant observations, experiments, mathematics, established theories, and philosophical or theological arguments; recover their assumptions, limits, and genuine points of disagreement.
2. **Separate and reconstitute ideas.** Distinguish transferable conceptual structure from a field's conventional interpretation; examine whether ideas can be applied differently and connected within a globally coherent account. The *new arrangement and relationships* may themselves be part of the intellectual contribution even when the components are familiar.
3. **Develop new explanatory ideas.** Where the available knowledge leaves a gap, formulate candidate concepts or hypotheses, consider competing accounts, and test their implications and coherence. An original proposal must not be mistaken for a confirmed result.

The project uses **Open-Thetical Research (OTR)**: a coherence-guided, recursively adaptive form of directed exploration. It chooses bounded inquiries for their *information value*, not because they are expected to confirm a favoured outcome. Scientific rounds declare their rules, controls, measures and stopping conditions before execution; findings, including failures and artefacts, inform the next independently specified round. See the [OTR method](research-methods/open-thetical-research.md) and [shared methods register](research-methods/README.md).

### Author, AI and review responsibilities

The **author retains intellectual authority** over the framework, its conceptual organisation, and substantive ontological decisions. AI assists with research, comparison, critical debate, logic checking, and the execution of *agreed* editorial changes. Neither an AI's familiarity with a conventional interpretation nor its unfamiliarity with a novel arrangement decides whether an argument is sound.

A good review first reconstructs the proposal *on its own terms*, then challenges its logic, assumptions, alternatives, dependencies and consequences. Reviewer objections are themselves subject to criticism. Results of review have different destinations:

- **Established error:** agree the intellectual correction, then implement and verify it precisely.
- **Disputed criticism:** examine the disagreement without silently changing the thesis.
- **Open research question:** preserve the alternatives, uncertainty and possible discriminating tests, without forcing a conclusion.

The objective is **discovery, disciplined synthesis, invention, challenge and controlled revision**, not machine-generated consensus.

These programmes investigate **formal possibilities and their implications**. A computational demonstration that a specified rule produces a specified pattern is evidence about *that model*. It does not, by itself, show that physical matter, quantum theory, gravity or time is generated by the Relational Substrate. Negative results, dependency on imposed scaffolds, shared code ancestry and failures of proposed analogies are part of the record, not material to be hidden.

**Current evidential position:** the project has developed a substantial conceptual framework and reproducible, bounded model investigations. The scriptural premise concerning the seen and the unseen is a theological starting commitment; the **specific relational account of material constitution remains an ontological hypothesis**, with its detailed mechanism unresolved. Neither the models nor the success of quantum mathematics establish that particular mechanism, and the research has not derived a validated replacement for established physical theory. This does **not** make experimental proof of ultimate ontology a prerequisite for rigorous philosophical inquiry. The [federated map](research-synthesis/generated/index.md) records unresolved comparisons and limitations. Claims in individual reports must always be read at their stated scope.

## Earlier work and repository history

The project has changed materially as its questions and evidence have developed. Earlier modelling explored browser-based relational grammars, directional order effects, and a more specific **vorton/topological** candidate for the structure of matter. Those records matter as research history, including their successes, failures, assumptions and unresolved tests. **They are not the present ontology's foundational commitments**, and earlier declarations about the completion of a *specific* conceptual or instantiation phase must not be read as a claim that the current global-coherence programme is complete.

The older sandbox and model-analysis tooling remain in this repository. For that **historical** browser demonstration, use:

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Its visual geometry is a modelling aid, not a literal depiction of substrate entities. See the [earlier conceptual-phase record](docs/conceptual-phase-completion-2026-07-12.md), [decision log](docs/decision-log.md), and [sandbox guide](docs/sandbox-and-benchmarks.md). The separate research branches preserve the historical work under their own provenance and archive arrangements.
